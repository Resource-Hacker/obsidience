# Getting help

- **Questions, setup help and ideas:** ask in
  [Discussions](https://github.com/Resource-Hacker/obsidience/discussions).
  Search first; someone may have asked already.
- **Bugs:** open a
  [bug report](https://github.com/Resource-Hacker/obsidience/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml)
  with your hardware, distribution and compositor, and the steps to reproduce.
- **Feature requests:** open a
  [feature request](https://github.com/Resource-Hacker/obsidience/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml)
  once the idea is concrete; open-ended ideas fit Discussions better.
- **Security problems:** report them privately as described in
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), never in a public issue.

Before asking, the [documentation site](https://resource-hacker.github.io/obsidience/),
the [README](README.md), [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md), the
[Shell README](obsidience/shell/README.md) and the
[Architecture](obsidience/defaults/vault/Architecture/Architecture.md) Articles
answer many questions about setup and behavior.

Obsidience is a development project, not a universal desktop installer, and it
is maintained on a best-effort basis. Logs, the exact commit and the relevant
`obsidience status` output make questions much easier to answer; leave out
secrets and personal data.
