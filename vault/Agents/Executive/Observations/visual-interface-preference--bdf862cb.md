title: Visual interface preference
---

The owner prefers readable terminal-like typography, restrained colored glow,
arc-reactor styling, clear role icons, and an information-rich but legible
knowledge graph. The graph should reveal the actual hierarchy and live
activation path rather than decorate the screen with synthetic activity.

Reader articles, Source code, Tasks, Library, and Review should use calm spacing,
human labels, and consistent capitalization. Pane modules may dock, stack,
collapse, or detach while Reader remains the single center viewing surface.
