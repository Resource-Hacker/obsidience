How to propose vault changes that pass review.

- One concern per proposal; several small proposals beat one large one.
- `update` proposals must contain the complete corrected body — the review
  applies it verbatim, so a partial body would destroy the rest of the note.
- The reason must cite evidence notes as [[wikilinks]] and fit in two
  sentences. If you cannot argue it in two sentences, do not propose it.
- Never propose edits to a note you have not read in this session.
