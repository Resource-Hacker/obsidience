title: detect-contradictions
kind: runbook
skills: ["[[Skills/reading-the-vault]]", "[[Skills/proposing-changes]]"]
---
Find statements in the vault that contradict each other.

1. Pick one subject area from a `vault.search` sweep (vary terms).
2. Read the full notes; list claims that cannot all be true.
3. For each contradiction, stage ONE `vault.propose` that flags both notes in
   its reason and proposes the correction you can best argue for.
4. No contradictions found is a valid completed result — say what you checked.
