title: 'Teamfight Tactics Patch 17.9: Final Space Gods Update'
---

Teamfight Tactics Patch 17.9 is the final dedicated update for the 'Space Gods' set, arriving before the 'Enchanted Wilds' set launch on August 26th. The update focuses on champion and augment balancing to stabilize the meta, specifically targeting economy and combat augments.

Notable champion changes include Maokai’s base attack speed increasing from 0.6 to 0.7 and NOVA Selector maximum-health damage increasing from 8% to 12%, while Rhaast’s spell duration drops from 2 seconds to 1 second. The official notes remain authoritative for the complete Gwen, Lulu, Tahm Kench, Twisted Fate, Milio, Meepsie, Zed, and augment changes.
