title: 'World of Warcraft: The War Within Expansion Overview'
---

The World of Warcraft: The War Within expansion marks the beginning of 'The Worldsoul Saga' story arc. Key features of the expansion include Delves (dynamic, small-group content), Warbands (account-wide progression system), new zones like the Isle of Dorn, and Hero Talents for class customization.
