title: 'WoW Midnight Season 2 Tuning: August 18th Wave'
---

The first wave of class tuning for World of Warcraft: Midnight Season 2 (Patch 12.1) has been announced for the weekly reset on August 18, 2026. These changes focus on balancing performance across classes and roles.

### Key Tuning Changes

* **Discipline Priest:** Damage output is being significantly reduced (by approximately 30%), with Atonement being buffed to maintain neutral healing output.
* **Arcane Mage:** The 2-set bonus on Arcane Missiles is being reduced from 20% to 5%.
* **Blood Death Knight:** Noted as a recent meta winner, subject to tuning-related adjustments in this wave.

Additional tuning passes are anticipated for September 2026.

The published Season 2 roadmap places this tuning wave alongside the August 18 opening of Normal, Heroic, and Mythic Venomous Abyss raid difficulties, with additional tuning passes expected as the season develops.
