Patch 12.0.7, titled 'Revelations,' was released on June 16, 2026, as part of the Midnight expansion.

### Key Content and Features
- **New Zones:** Adds two new Void Worlds, Naigtal and Val, accessible via a rotating portal in Voidstorm. These zones include a Heroic World Tier.
- **New Raid:** Introduces the Sporefall raid.
- **Omnium Folio System:** A new power system involving Runes that provides player power boosts and gameplay enhancements.
- **New Currency:** Field Accolades (seasonal currency).
- **Timewalking:** Introduces Turbulent Timeways, which features six Dragonflight dungeons.
- **Storyline:** Focuses on the war against the Void and the Zul'jan storyline.
- **Additional Updates:** Includes catch-up gearing and various quality-of-life improvements.
