---

Blizzard has introduced several systemic changes in the first Patch 12.1 PTR build for Midnight Season 2 to smooth out combat and improve feedback.

### Key Combat Adjustments
- **Damage Smoothing**: Player health and enemy damage are both increased by 25%. This change aims to make incoming damage less "spiky" and provide more consistent combat pacing.
- **DPS Cooldown Tuning**: The impact of DPS cooldowns is being reduced/smoothed out to mitigate extreme burst windows.
- **Interrupt Feedback**: A new visual and audio indicator will trigger when a player misses an interrupt, providing immediate feedback on failed mechanics.

### Context
These adjustments are part of the initial tuning phase for the Patch 12.1 PTR build, focusing on combat stability and player feedback during the Midnight expansion.
