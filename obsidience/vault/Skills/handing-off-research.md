---
title: Hand off research
kind: skill
tool: '[[Tools/source.handoff]]'
---
Use `source.handoff` once after the Research outcome is self-contained. Give
the finding a short subject title and include the answer, material dates,
direct references, uncertainty, limitations, and every exact `source://`
citation that supports it.

Do not hand off a transcript, source dump, unsupported conclusion, or proposed
wiki path. The physical Inbox is transit Source for Alexandria, not accepted
Knowledge and not an owner Review queue. If the evidence does not support a
useful finding, complete the Research Task honestly without creating a
handoff.
