## Summary

The BBC's Middle East Correspondent, Lucy Williamson, visited the Palestinian community of Khan al Ahmar in the so-called E1 area of the West Bank, and the neighbouring Israeli settlement of Maale Admumim.

The report focuses on the uncertain status of this land, which Israel has earmarked for settlement development while the area remains in limbo — not yet officially designated as part of either Israeli or Palestinian territory.
