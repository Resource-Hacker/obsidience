## Police and army attend funeral for officer killed in wrong-way A66 crash

**Published:** 10 September 2026, 15:02 GMT | **Source:** [BBC News](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cnvl72e57yeo)

**Summary**

Former army colleagues, police officers, and emergency workers gathered at Kirkleatham Walled Gardens in Redcar for the funeral of PC Tom Clough, 38, who died in a head-on collision on the A66 near Middlesbrough on 22 August 2026.

**Key facts**

- PC Clough and his colleague PC Matthew Blades were killed when a Volkswagen Passat struck their marked police vehicle in the early hours of 22 August. Five young men in the Passat also died.
- Clough served in the British Army (2006–2012) as a lance corporal with the Royal Logistics Corps, specifically with 11 Explosive Ordnance Disposal and Search Regiment. After leaving the Army, he worked at the Civil Nuclear Constabulary in Sellafield, then the British Transport Police, before joining Cleveland Police as an armed response officer.
- Officers had been pursuing the Passat seconds before the crash after its cloned registration plates triggered an ANPR camera, though they were not involved in the pursuit (per the Independent Office for Police Conduct).
- A mounted police unit of four horses led the procession, followed by the hearse carrying Clough's coffin (draped with the Union Flag and topped with his custodian helmet), flanked by officers in ceremonial dress. The cortege was accompanied by a lone piper.
- Cleveland Police Federation chair Lauren Somerville said Clough's partner described the public support as bringing "so much strength." She reported his partner said Clough "was described as giving his everything to everybody," was "a partner and a brother and a son and most importantly a father to a sausage dog called Frank, who was the love of his life."
- Policing minister Sarah Jones, local MP Anna Turley, and Tees Valley Mayor Ben Houchen attended.
- More than £1.2m was raised via a Cleveland Police Federation fundraiser to help the families of both PC Blades and PC Clough.
