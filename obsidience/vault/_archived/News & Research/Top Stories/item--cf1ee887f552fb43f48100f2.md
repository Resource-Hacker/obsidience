**What happened**: About 30–40 people congregated at the Marriott Hotel in Cosham, Portsmouth, on Tuesday evening after false rumours circulated online that a coach carrying migrants had arrived. Protesters then entered the hotel floor where the Pakistan under‑19 cricket team was staying. Hampshire Police found no offences committed and the crowd dispersed by about 21:00 BST.

**What was actually there**: The guests were members of the Pakistan under‑19 cricket team. Reform county councillor George Madgwick said hotel staff showed him proof and that a photo of people "looking like foreign nationals" leaving the coach, posted online, had sparked the misinformation. Madgwick denied the reaction was racist, describing it as a "grave mistake".  

**Responses**: Sumair Syed, chief operating officer of the Pakistan Cricket Board, said the team reported people "knocking on their doors" but the players "behaved responsibly and didn't escalate". He called the incident "unfortunate". The ECB said it has checked on the squad's wellbeing and is reviewing security for the remainder of the tour. Labour MP Amanda Martin called it "deeply concerning" and "completely unacceptable".  

**Context**: The incident came two days after hundreds of protesters blocked roads in Portsmouth after a boat carrying around 120 migrants was brought ashore in the city.
