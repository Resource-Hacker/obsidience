On 9 September 2026, the BBC's Middle East Correspondent Lucy Williamson visited the Palestinian community of Khan al Ahmar in the so-called E1 area of the West Bank, as well as the neighbouring Israeli settlement of Maale Admumim.

Context: UK sanctions on illegal Israeli settlements in the occupied West Bank triggered a series of Israeli counter-measures against UK nationals and organisations. The sanctions followed Israel's tendering of construction projects for new settlement homes in the strategically important E1 area east of Jerusalem — plans that drew widespread international condemnation. The development would effectively divide the West Bank in two and isolate East Jerusalem.
