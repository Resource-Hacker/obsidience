On 9 September 2026, police officers gave PC Matthew Blades a guard of honour as people lined the streets for his funeral. The officer died in a wrong-way collision on the A66.

Officers stood as a guard of honour as the hearse passed by, with hundreds of people lining the streets to pay their respects. PC Blades had served with the force for 33 years.

The crash involved a wrong-way driver on the A66, where officers are known to patrol for speeders. It remains unclear whether the officer was a passenger or driver at the time of the incident.

The funeral proceedings reflected the significant impact of his death on the local community, with the formal honour marking the end of his 33-year policing career.
