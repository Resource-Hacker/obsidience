Following weeks of national dialogue with around 4,000 delegates in Addis Ababa, Ethiopian authorities are considering merging the prime minister and president roles into a single presidential system. PM Abiy Ahmed, who won a massive parliamentary majority in June, could benefit from the reform. Critics note opposition parties boycotted the process and question whether the concentration of power reflects popular will.
