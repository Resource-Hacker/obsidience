Nepal marked a national day of mourning with flags at half-mast as rescue teams continue searching for survivors of a glacial flash flood. At least 1,287 people are confirmed dead and over 5,000 remain missing. Rescuers are intensifying operations in hydropower tunnels where hundreds of workers are believed trapped. On the Tibetan side, 31 people died with 531 unaccounted for. A 64-year-old woman was rescued more than 10 days after being buried.
