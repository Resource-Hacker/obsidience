Reported 7 Sept 2026. US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner held "encouraging" talks in Kyiv on Sunday with President Zelenskyy during their first official visit to Ukraine, after meeting Putin in Moscow the day prior. No breakthrough was announced on territorial concessions or other key issues. Zelenskyy said talks focused on military aid, including Patriot systems, and said Kyiv will need a "winter package" if fighting continues. Putin had declared a three-day ceasefire for the envoys' safety.
