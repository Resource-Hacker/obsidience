Kenyan authorities have begun enforcing a crackdown on foreign nationals working as informal hawkers, following President William Ruto's call to reserve small-scale trading for citizens. The move, framed as economic protection, has drawn mixed reactions and xenophobia concerns. Ruto also proposed a Local Content Bill requiring foreign companies to source 60 percent of goods locally and employ 80 percent Kenyan workers.
