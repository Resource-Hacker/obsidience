President Trump issued executive orders banning imports of Canadian spirits, some dairy products, non-alcoholic beer and motorbikes beginning 29 September, after Canadian retaliatory tariffs on US goods took effect. Canada's trade minister called the measures unjustified. The bans cover roughly 0.25% of Canadian exports to the US, but economists say they signal the trade war is about inflicting economic pain rather than raising revenue.
