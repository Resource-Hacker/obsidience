Kenyan authorities began enforcing a policy reserving small-scale hawking and petty retail for citizens, following President Ruto's order. The crackdown targets undocumented foreign nationals in an informal sector employing 17.4 million people. Analysts warn it may scare investors ahead of elections, while the government insists legally documented traders remain protected. Kenya attracted a record $3.2 billion in foreign direct investment in 2025.
