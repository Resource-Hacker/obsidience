Kenya opened a 90-day registration window for undocumented foreign nationals after President Ruto's directive targeting foreigners in small-scale businesses sparked panic among Burundians. Hundreds scrambled for emergency travel documents. A senior official apologised to Burundians, saying the directive had been misunderstood. The government warned that no group has authority to harass foreign nationals. Burundi's foreign minister summoned East African Community representatives to address rising regional tensions.
