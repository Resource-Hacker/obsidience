Hungary's new prime minister, Peter Magyar, has begun a drive to recover billions that he says were stolen or diverted by the previous Orban administration. Magyar promised during his campaign to reclaim those government funds. A newly created National Asset Recovery Authority with broad policing and prosecutorial powers is set to focus primarily on the Orban era, and its success or failure is expected to have a major impact on the new government's central pledge.
