Former Bosnian Serb commander Ratko Mladic, convicted of genocide in Srebrenica, was buried in Belgrade with military honors on Monday. President Vučić did not attend, citing a scheduled visit, and dismissed EU criticism as "stupid." The funeral comes days before Vučić is expected to call a snap election, with analysts saying it is being used to stoke nationalism ahead of the vote.
