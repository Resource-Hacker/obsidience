Reported 7 Sept 2026. Public hearings opened at the International Court of Justice in The Hague as Germany asked the court to dismiss Nicaragua's case alleging Berlin violated the 1948 Genocide Convention by supplying weapons to Israel. Germany argued procedural defects and jurisdictional issues, while noting its export controls exceed international standards. Bundestag records show roughly €167 million in approved exports between November 2025 and March 2026. A decision on Germany's preliminary request to dismiss the case is tentatively expected this year; a full hearing, if pursued, could take years.
