The UN General Assembly voted to replace the traditional world map with one that more accurately reflects Africa’s size. The Togo-sponsored “Correct the Map” resolution was backed by 164 nations, including France and the UK; the United States was the only nation to vote against, with six abstentions. The non-binding vote is expected to influence maps used by institutions worldwide and responds to criticism that standard projections such as Mercator shrink Africa’s apparent landmass.
