Prime Minister Peter Magyar's administration has begun asset-recovery operations aimed at funds and property diverted during Viktor Orbán's tenure. The National Asset Recovery Authority (NVVH), set up late July, holds policing and prosecutorial powers and is accountable to parliament. Magyar cited around €60 billion, though other estimates are higher. Parliament late last week appointed political scientist Anna Unger to head the NVVH, drawing controversy; she cautioned that legally binding verdicts in major cases were not expected within its first six-year term.
