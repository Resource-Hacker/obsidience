The NTSB recovered flight data and cockpit voice recorders from the Boeing 767-300 that overshot a runway at Miami International Airport on Sunday, killing five people and seriously injuring five. The plane, operated by 21 Air for Amazon, struck a van and a car before coming to rest 394 m beyond the runway. NTSB chairwoman Homendy called the scene "devastating" and said victim recovery was the priority.
