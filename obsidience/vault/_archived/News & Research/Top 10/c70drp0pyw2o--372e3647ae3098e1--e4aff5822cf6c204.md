Two thieves broke into the Renoir Museum in Cagnes-sur-Mer in southern France, seizing four paintings but dropping two in the gardens as they fled. The missing works are Portrait of Madame Colonna Romano (1910) and Jeune fille au puits (1886), each valued at €9m. The mayor described the suspects as well-equipped and well-informed, wearing helmets, gloves and combat gear.
