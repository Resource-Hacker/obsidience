German police arrested a 48-year-old man carrying explosives near the Weisweiler power plant in western Germany, suspected of a series of sabotage attacks on electricity substations in Brandenburg, Saxony and North Rhine-Westphalia. The attacks used rockets to shoot wires across high-voltage cables causing short circuits; in Saxony alone, 21 devices were found near power lines. Interior Minister Schuster admitted the incidents could have led to a regional blackout but warned against jumping to conclusions about motive.
