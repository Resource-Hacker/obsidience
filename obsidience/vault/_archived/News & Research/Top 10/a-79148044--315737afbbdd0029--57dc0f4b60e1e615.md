Germany asked the International Court of Justice to dismiss Nicaragua's case accusing Berlin of aiding genocide in Gaza through arms sales. Germany argued procedural deficiencies and lack of jurisdiction. Between November 2025 and March 2026, exports worth approximately €167 million were approved. Nicaragua is expected to present its arguments on Tuesday; a decision on Germany's bid is tentatively expected this year.
