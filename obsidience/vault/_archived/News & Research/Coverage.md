Coverage comes from the owner's configured Connections and Feeds. Each Feed selects the provider endpoint, collection amount, destination, interval, retention limit and optional instructions for Darwin. Publisher ordering and timestamps are evidence about that provider's selection, not an objective ranking of world events.

[Distill](/Tasks/research/distill.md) reads one captured Feed item completely and fetches only its exact reporting page when the item lacks necessary substance. Darwin preserves the supplied meaning, reporting date, qualifications, attribution and Source citations, then sends one complete summary to Alexandria's Inbox. A feed excerpt is labelled as captured provider material rather than a fetched full article.

Publication time, event time and capture time remain distinct. Reporting from one publisher is attributed rather than presented as independent confirmation. Failed acquisitions supply no evidence. The owner-selected node and its Auto-curate policy govern publication through [Ingest](/Tasks/ingest.md); the Feed's active-Article limit governs bounded archival.

[News & Research](/News%20%26%20Research/News%20%26%20Research.md) remains the maintained collection. [Top Stories](/News%20%26%20Research/Top%20Stories/Top%20Stories.md) receives new and updated BBC Top Stories Feed items. The former Top 10 edition is archived; earlier Feed publications retain their original placement and normal retention eligibility. New Feed items use their captured lineage and current Feed policy. Raw Sources and prior handoffs remain available for explicit historical inspection.
