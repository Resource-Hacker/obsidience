Ula'tek is the final boss of the Venomous Abyss raid in World of Warcraft: Patch 12.1. The fight is a three-phase encounter with an intermission between phases two and three.

**Key Mechanics:**
- **Caustic Waves:** Ula'tek sends waves of venom across the platform, dealing immediate Nature damage and then periodically for 10 seconds. Players must avoid the paths of these waves.
- **Circling Prey:** An area-denial mechanic that progressively restricts safe space. Players must maintain medium range from the boss. maintaining a consistent placement is key to managing the safe space.
- **Add Spawns:** Constant add spawns occur throughout the fight.

**Strategy Summary:**
- **Arena Control:** Arena management is crucial due to continuous area denial (Caustic Waves and Circling Prey).
