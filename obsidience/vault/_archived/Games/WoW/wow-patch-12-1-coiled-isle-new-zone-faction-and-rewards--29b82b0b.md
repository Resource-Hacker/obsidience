title: 'World of Warcraft patch 12.1 Coiled Isle: zone, faction, and rewards'
---

Patch 12.1 (The Curse of Ula'tek) continues the Midnight expansion campaign with new activities in the Coiled Isle zone. Key features include the new Zul'jarra's Forces faction with a Renown track up to level 20, existing content like the Vaults of Atal’Utek, and various collectibles including the Ruby Writhe mount.

Renown 5 unlocks a significant set of new profession recipes, including alchemy and fishing rewards. Players pursuing Patch 12.1 profession progression should prioritize Zul’jarra’s Forces reputation.
