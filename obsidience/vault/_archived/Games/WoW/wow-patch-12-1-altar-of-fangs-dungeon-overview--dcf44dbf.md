Altar of Fangs is a three-boss dungeon introduced in Patch 12.1 (Midnight expansion) and part of the Season 2 Mythic+ rotation. It is located within the Vaults of Atal’Utek on the Coiled Isle.

**Boss Order and Key Mechanics:**
1. **Rav'i:** A primary encounter testing shield-breaking and floor awareness. Key mechanic involves **Carrion Piles**, which Rav'i uses to feed and gain a protective shield.
2. **The Writhing Coil:** (Mechanics not explicitly detailed in summary, but follows Rav'i).
3. **Zul'jan:** (Mechanics not explicitly detailed in summary, but follows The Writhing Coil).

**General Dungeon Info:**
- **Location:** The Coiled Isle, beneath the Vaults of Atal’Utek.
- **Difficulty:** Normal, Heroic, Mythic, and Mythic+.
- **Theme:** Cursed troll temple focused on venom and serpent-worship mechanics.
