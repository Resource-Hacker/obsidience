Blizzard’s Curse of Ula’tek content update is scheduled to go live August 11, 2026. It continues the Midnight campaign on the Coiled Isle and adds the eight-boss Venomous Abyss raid, the three-boss Altar of Fangs dungeon, instanced Lairs with scalable world-boss encounters, and three Delves: The Ring of Glory, Gnarldor Isle, and the Venomfall Deeps Nemesis Delve.

Midnight Season 2 begins one week after the content update. It brings the raid season, Mythic+ rotation changes, PvP and Prey updates, and upper-tier Bountiful Delves. This is patch-content context; Squancher’s live character state, gear, and talents still come from the addon and Battle.net tools rather than web summaries.
