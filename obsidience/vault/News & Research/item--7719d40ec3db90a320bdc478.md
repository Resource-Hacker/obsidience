## RNLI volunteer targeted online and branded 'traitor' after Portsmouth protests

**Reporting date:** 9 September 2026

An RNLI volunteer has come forward to speak with BBC Verify after being wrongly identified as having helped small boat migrants during protests in Portsmouth, and subsequently targeted online with accusations that he is a 'traitor'.

The volunteer, whose identity has been confirmed, denies ever having assisted migrants in any way. He became embroiled in the controversy when reports circulated identifying him as someone who had helped, despite his clear denial of any such involvement.

Following the false identification, the volunteer reports being 'targeted online' and branded a traitor, reflecting the intense public debate around migrant rescue operations and the role of rescue services.
