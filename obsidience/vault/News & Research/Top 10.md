## 1. Nepal-Tibet flood: two workers pulled alive from hydropower tunnel
Nine days after glacial-collapse floods swept a Himalayan border valley, Nepali rescuers hauled two injured workers from the Trishuli 3A tunnel and found a third body. BBC reports 1,311 confirmed dead and 5,330 missing across Nepal and Tibet (country breakdown: Nepal 1,287 + Tibet 31), with roughly 39 believed trapped in that tunnel - not all confirmed alive. ## 2. US strikes three Iranian oil tankers after warships targeted
US Centcom said it hit three Iran-linked tankers (near Kharg Island and near Jask in the Gulf of Oman) after IRGC ballistic missiles targeted a US carrier and destroyer, claiming it "permanently disabled" two and "destroyed" a third and that it "evaded" the missile attacks with no US troops harmed. Iran's military warned of expanded retaliation; IRIB reported no casualties near Kharg. Centcom calls the tankers part of a "shadow network" funding the IRGC. Report date 5 September 2026. ## 3. US envoys meet Putin as Ukraine peace push accelerates
Witkoff and Kushner met Putin at the Kremlin, where a three-day mutual halt on strikes between Moscow and Kyiv was agreed and a US war-ending proposal referenced. The envoys were to fly to Kyiv Sunday for their first in-country talks; the Moscow-to-Kyiv route is unclear because Ukrainian airspace has been closed since 2022. Report date 5 September 2026. ## 4. Bolivia: explosion at Viacha military barracks kills at least two
"Pyrotechnic material" detonated at an artillery barracks about 30 km from La Paz, killing at least two, injuring around 81, and leaving 14 missing; a health official (via Reuters) cited a possible 10-15 death toll. Police said fireworks were stored on site, homes were damaged, and warned of further blasts; the cause is under investigation. Report date 5 September 2026. ## 5. Russia drones Ukrainian security headquarters in Kyiv

The strike followed overnight "demonstrative" strikes on Kyiv and Boryspil airports, which Zelensky linked to plans for the US team to land in the capital. Report date 4 September 2026. ## 6. Europe faces escalating sabotage campaign; Russia chief suspect
Germany's interior minister declared Russia responsible for a failed drone attack on Leipzig airport, the latest in an August string of fires and incendiary strikes on defence sites (Bulgaria, Italy, Estonia, Slovakia, Poland). Russia calls the accusation "absurd"; analysts describe "coercive signalling" to pressure European backers of Ukraine, and some fires may be ruled accidental. Report date 4 September 2026. ## 7. Germany's far-right AfD hunts first post-war state government
In the Saxony-Anhalt vote, the AfD targets an outright parliamentary majority that would make it the first far-right party to hold state power in post-war Germany; it leads the polls and seeks to break the "firewall" against coalitionising with it. The result is a test for Chancellor Friedrich Merz. Vote Sunday. Report date 5 September 2026. URL: https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cvgypkzgy4wo - source://dda8df70-c796-4b2a-9619-eb0ef8fb928b

## 8. UN votes to replace Mercator world map
The UN General Assembly passed Togo's "Correct the Map" resolution, backed by 164 nations including France and the UK, with the US the sole vote against (six abstentions), adopting the Equal Earth projection over the centuries-old Mercator. The vote is non-binding; critics say Mercator distorts Africa's size. Report date 4 September 2026 (updated 5 September 2026). URL: https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ce30vp55dnlo - source://6584745b-a7ee-4560-acbf-01754b721ba8

## 9. Yemen: Houthi forces push west as fighting intensifies
Houthi forces are pressing to secure high ground and consolidate control of the Red Sea entry (Bab el-Mandeb) in western Yemen after the July truce collapsed amid the wider US-Iran conflict. AFP tallied 129 deaths as of Friday (both sides suffered "dozens" of losses, reported off the record); the fight raises regional shipping and security concerns. Report date 4 September 2026. URL: https://www.dw.com/en/yemen-fighting-intensifies-amid-houthi-push-in-the-west/a-79039588 - source://e13a90c7-4f1e-4922-92a1-8eb1ada82b71

## 10. Nigeria: at least 37 suspected oil thieves die in ship incident
The NGO Youth and Advocacy Centre says at least 37 people died after inhaling fumes while illegally siphoning crude from a loading vessel in Okrika, Rivers State; police confirmed deaths / a "fatal incident" under investigation, and the NSCDC called the circulating figures unverified. If confirmed, it would be the worst such oil-theft accident since at least October 2023. Report date 4 September 2026.
