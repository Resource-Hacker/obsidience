Odesa, Ukraine's third-biggest and overwhelmingly Russian-speaking city, will decide this week whether to back a plan halting Russian-language music and literature in public spaces and state arts venues, adding to national restrictions. Supporters frame it as decolonising from Russian influence; critics say it alienates native Russian speakers in a city under daily strikes. The outcome is a test case for wartime language policy.
