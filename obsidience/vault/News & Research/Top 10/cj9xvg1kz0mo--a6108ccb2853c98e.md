The first road bridge linking Russia and North Korea across the Tumen river opened, allowing up to 300 vehicles and 2,323 people daily. Ukrainian investigators say it serves as a covert military logistics corridor for Russia's war in Ukraine. South Korean estimates put North Korean weapons transfers at $7–14 billion, with roughly 11,000 North Korean troops fighting in Russia. Construction took 495 days.
