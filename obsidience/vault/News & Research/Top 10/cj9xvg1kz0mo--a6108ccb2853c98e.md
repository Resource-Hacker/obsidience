The first road bridge across the Tumen river connecting Russia and North Korea has opened, allowing up to 300 vehicles and 2,323 people daily. Ukrainian investigators say it is primarily a covert military logistics corridor. Construction took 495 days after being agreed during Putin's 2024 visit to Pyongyang. An estimated 11,000 North Korean troops are fighting in Ukraine, and $7–14 billion in weapons have already been transferred.
