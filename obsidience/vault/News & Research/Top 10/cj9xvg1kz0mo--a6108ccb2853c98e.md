The first road bridge linking Russia and North Korea across the Tumen river has opened, allowing up to 300 vehicles and 2,323 people daily. Russian PM Mishustin called it a "new symbol of friendship." Ukrainian investigative group Truth Hounds says the bridge is primarily a covert military logistics corridor. South Korean intelligence estimates North Korea has already transferred $7–14 billion in weapons to Russia, with an estimated 11,000 North Korean troops fighting in Ukraine. The bridge took 495 days to complete after construction began in 2025.
