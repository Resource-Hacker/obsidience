Egyptian television presenter Sarah Khalifa and 11 others were sentenced to death by hanging after conviction on drugs charges; nine fellow defendants received life and seven were acquitted. Khalifa's lawyer said she would appeal. The verdict, first announced the prior month, was confirmed only after the court obtained the grand mufti's required religious opinion.
