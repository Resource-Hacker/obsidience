An Egyptian court sentenced TV presenter Sarah Khalifa and 11 others to death by hanging after convictions on drugs charges, according to state-owned al-Ahram as reported by the BBC. Khalifa, known for Mission Impossible, denied the charges, and her lawyer said she would appeal. Nine other defendants received life sentences and seven were acquitted; the reported case involved imported drug ingredients, raw materials, and illegal firearms and munitions.
