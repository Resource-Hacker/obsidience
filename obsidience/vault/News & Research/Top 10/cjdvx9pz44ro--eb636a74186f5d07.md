BBC Verify analysis shows Kyiv endured 76 air raid alerts in the seven days from 27 August, the highest since the 2022 invasion began. Russia's new tactic of daytime strikes, combined with faster jet-powered drones flying up to 595 km/h, forces residents to shelter up to 13 times daily. Ukraine's air force reports intercepting only about 60 percent of the drones. The government is planning a two-tier warning system to reduce disruption to schools and businesses.
