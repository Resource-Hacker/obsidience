A Russian drone struck the We Are Ukraine TV channel headquarters in Kyiv on Tuesday while journalists were broadcasting live, President Zelensky said. Overnight and daytime strikes killed at least five people across the capital and injured 25. The attack came despite a three-day capital-strike pause agreed while US envoys visited. Zelensky called it a "new page in Russian degradation." BBC Verify found Kyiv is suffering more daily air raid alerts than any point since 2022.
