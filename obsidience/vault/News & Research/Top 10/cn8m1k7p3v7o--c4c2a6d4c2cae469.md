The attack came after both sides had agreed to halt strikes on each other's capitals for three days while US envoys visited Moscow and Kyiv. President Zelensky said he expected the war to continue into winter following talks with Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner that ended without major breakthroughs. A BBC Verify analysis found Kyiv is suffering more daily air raid alerts than at any point since 2022.
