President Zelensky said the war with Russia was likely to continue into winter after talks in Kyiv with US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner, who had met President Putin in Moscow the previous day. The discussions ended without a major breakthrough, though Witkoff said he was encouraged by substantive talks. Zelensky said Ukraine needed winter air-defence and energy support and called for a trilateral meeting with Russia and the United States while both sides continued exchanging strikes.
