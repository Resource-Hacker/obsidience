Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky said the war with Russia was expected to continue into winter after hours of talks in Kyiv with US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner, who had met Vladimir Putin in Moscow the previous day. No major breakthrough was announced; Witkoff called the talks “substantive.” The sides agreed that officials from Ukraine, Europe, and the US would meet soon, and Putin and Zelensky agreed to a three-day halt to strikes on each other’s capitals.
