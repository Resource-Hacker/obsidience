Canada's retaliatory tariffs on nearly C$28bn ($20bn) of American goods came into force on Tuesday, applying rates up to 50% to items from steel to furniture. The move follows the collapse of US-Canada trade talks in late August and escalates the world's largest bilateral trading relationship. Prime Minister Mark Carney said Canada seeks a "durable" deal but no movement to resume negotiations has been made. US trade representative Jamieson Greer said the "ball is in Canada's court." Around 41,000 Canadian jobs were lost in August amid the dispute.
