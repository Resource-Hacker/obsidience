At least 14 people were killed and 11 wounded when an explosion struck a temporary weapons storage site near Sarmada in Syria's northwestern Idlib province. The Defense Ministry said weapons and war remnants were being stored there before relocation. Secondary explosions and fires hampered emergency response. Authorities have not yet determined the cause. It was the second fatal incident of its kind in Idlib this month.
