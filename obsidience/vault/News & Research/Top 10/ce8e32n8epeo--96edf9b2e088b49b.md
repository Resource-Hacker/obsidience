The NTSB recovered flight data and cockpit voice recorders from the Boeing 767-300 that overshot the runway at Miami International Airport, killing five people. The aircraft, operated by 21 Air for Amazon, struck a cleaning van and a Toyota before coming to rest 1,300 feet beyond the runway. Investigators are in the fact-finding stage, examining weather, crew experience, and whether the runway should have had an arresting system.
