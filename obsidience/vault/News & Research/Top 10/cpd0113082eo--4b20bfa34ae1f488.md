The AfD won 43.8% of the vote in Saxony-Anhalt, falling three seats short of an outright majority. Deputy leader Beatrix von Storch urged opponents to enter government talks, saying "democracy demands" parties work with them. Chancellor Friedrich Merz called the result "seismic." No far-right party has controlled a German state since World War Two. The party is exploring a minority deal backed by the left-wing populist BSW.
