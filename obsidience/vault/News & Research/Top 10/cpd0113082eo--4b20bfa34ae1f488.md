The far-right AfD is "very, very close" to governing Saxony-Anhalt after winning 43.8% of the vote but falling three seats short of a majority. Deputy leader Beatrix von Storch called on opponents to enter talks, saying "democracy demands" cooperation. Chancellor Merz called the result "seismic." No far-right party has controlled a German state since World War Two.
