The AfD won 43.8% of the vote in Saxony-Anhalt's state election, falling three seats short of an outright majority. Its deputy leader Beatrix von Storch told the BBC that "democracy demands" other parties work with them. Chancellor Merz described the result as "seismic" and the CDU's worst defeat in decades. No far-right party has controlled a German state since World War Two. The party seeks a minority government backed by individuals or the left-wing BSW.
