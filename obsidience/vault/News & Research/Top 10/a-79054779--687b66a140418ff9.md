US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner held talks with Ukrainian President Zelenskyy in Kyiv on Sunday during their first official visit to Ukraine, following a meeting with Putin in Moscow the day before. Zelenskyy said the discussions focused on military aid including Patriot missile systems and that Ukraine would need a winter package if the war drags on. Kushner acknowledged the difficulty of bridging territorial differences, saying the US was working behind the scenes to create conditions for a possible deal.
