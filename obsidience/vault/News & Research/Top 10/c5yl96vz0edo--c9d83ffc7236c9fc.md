Israeli air strikes on the village of Kfar Roummane in southern Lebanon killed at least 12 people, including two children and four women, according to the Lebanese health ministry. The strikes destroyed a residential building and hit a paramedic vehicle. The IDF said it targeted Hezbollah infrastructure in retaliation for a drone attack on its troops. Lebanese President Joseph Aoun said the escalation is jeopardising the June US-brokered ceasefire. At least 27 people have been killed in Israeli attacks across southern Lebanon in three days. Overall, at least 4,362 people have been killed in Lebanon during the conflict.
