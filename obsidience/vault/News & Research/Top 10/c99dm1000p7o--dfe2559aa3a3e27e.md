President Trump signed executive orders banning imports of Canadian alcoholic spirits, dairy products, non-alcoholic beer, motorbikes and other goods effective 29 September, calling Canada's trade practices "discriminating." The move follows Canada's retaliatory dollar-for-dollar tariffs on US steel, clothing and furniture that took effect the same day. No new trade talks have been scheduled since negotiations collapsed in late August.
