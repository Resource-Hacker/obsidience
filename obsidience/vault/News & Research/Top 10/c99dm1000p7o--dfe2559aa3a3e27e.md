US President Trump issued executive orders banning imports of Canadian alcoholic spirits, dairy goods, motorbikes and other products from 29 September, citing Canadian discrimination against US goods. The measures come as Canada's dollar-for-dollar counter-tariffs on US steel, clothing and furniture take effect. No new trade talks have been scheduled since negotiations collapsed in late August. Trade expert Deborah Elms estimates a modest impact of around $1bn in goods, though the broader trade war has unsettled businesses on both sides of the border.
