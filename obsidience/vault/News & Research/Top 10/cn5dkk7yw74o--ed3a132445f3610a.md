Thousands turned out in Belgrade for the funeral of Ratko Mladić, the former Bosnian Serb general convicted of genocide for his role in the 1995 Srebrenica massacre, which killed around 8,000 Bosniak men and boys. Mladić died aged 84 in a UN prison in The Hague. The EU denounced the "glorification" of a convicted war criminal. Serbia, an EU candidate, has seen stalled accession talks. Several Serbian ministers attended, and the funeral was led by the Serbian Orthodox Patriarch. In Sarajevo, victims' families expressed anguish at the ceremony.
