Around 170,000 passengers were stranded after the Anak Krakatau eruption forced Indonesian authorities to suspend operations at eight airports, delaying 1,558 flights, once ash was detected near Jakarta's Soekarno-Hatta. The transport ministry said airports would stay closed until 09:00 Monday; authorities issued no tsunami warning as ash spread toward West Java and Sumatra.
