Eruptions of Mount Anak Krakatau forced Indonesian authorities to temporarily suspend operations at eight airports, beginning with Jakarta's Soekarno-Hatta on Sunday morning after ash was detected in nearby airspace. The transport ministry said 1,558 flights were delayed, leaving around 170,000 passengers stranded; routes to Singapore, Doha, Sydney and Kuala Lumpur were hit. Authorities warned of respiratory, eye and skin risks and confirmed there was no tsunami warning. Reported 6 Sept 2026.
