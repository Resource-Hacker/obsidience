Eruptions from Mount Anak Krakatau, starting Friday night, sent ash over the Jakarta area, closing eight Indonesian airports including Soekarno-Hatta and delaying 1,558 flights. Authorities confirmed there was no tsunami warning; the ash also disrupted schools and fishing, with health officials urging face masks. Report date 2026-09-06 (BBC).
