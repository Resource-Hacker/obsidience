The eruption of Mount Anak Krakatau forced Indonesia to suspend operations at eight airports, stranding around 170,000 passengers and delaying 1,558 flights. Ash reached as high as 15,000m on the Sumatra side, disrupting routes to Singapore, Doha, Sydney and Kuala Lumpur. Authorities issued no tsunami warning but urged residents to wear masks. The volcano had been erupting since Friday night, with two more blasts early Sunday. Reported 6 Sept 2026.
