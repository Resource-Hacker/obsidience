The Anak Krakatau eruption forced Indonesia to suspend operations at eight airports, stranding around 170,000 passengers; Jakarta's Soekarno-Hatta closed first on Sunday after ash was detected nearby. The transport ministry said 1,558 flights were delayed, mainly affecting Singapore and other international routes including Doha, Sydney and Kuala Lumpur. BMKG reported ash reaching 20,000 ft (6,000 m) east on the Java side and 50,000 ft (15,000 m) west on the Sumatra side. No tsunami warning was issued, and residents were urged to wear masks.
