Mount Anak Krakatau's eruption, ongoing since Friday night with two more early Sunday, forced temporary closure of eight Indonesian airports after ash was detected near Jakarta's Soekarno-Hatta, with 1,558 flights delayed. BMKG reported ash reaching 6,000 m east on the Java side and 15,000 m west on the Sumatra side. The transport ministry said routes to Singapore, Doha, Sydney and Kuala Lumpur were hardest hit. No tsunami warning was issued.
