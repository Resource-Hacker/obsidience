The eruption of Anak Krakatau led Indonesian authorities to suspend operations at eight airports, stranding around 170,000 passengers and delaying 1,558 flights. Ash was detected near Jakarta's Soekarno-Hatta airport and spread toward West Java; authorities said there was no tsunami warning. The transport minister urged faster refunds, while the health minister advised people in affected areas to stay indoors and wear masks because of respiratory, eye, and skin risks.
