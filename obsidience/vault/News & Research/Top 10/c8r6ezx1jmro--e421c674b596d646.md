[https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c8r6ezx1jmro](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c8r6ezx1jmro)

A five-storey student hostel in south-west Delhi collapsed, with as many as 50 people believed to have been inside. Police told PTI that at least three people were killed and three others were in critical condition, while eight were rescued. Basement construction work was underway in the building near the Delhi University campus. Delhi's chief minister said an adjacent building was evacuated as a precaution and that every possible effort was being made to rescue those trapped.
