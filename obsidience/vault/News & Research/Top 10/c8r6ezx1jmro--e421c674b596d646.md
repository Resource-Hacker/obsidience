A five-storey student hostel, Hostel Daze, in Satya Niketan, south-west Delhi, collapsed around 13:30 local time on Sunday; eyewitnesses said up to 50 people were inside. PTI, citing police, reported at least three killed and three in critical condition, with at least eight rescued. Basement construction work was underway near the Delhi University campus; an adjacent hostel also collapsed and another building was evacuated as a precaution. Police are searching for the building owner; Delhi has recorded more than 1,000 building collapses in three years.
