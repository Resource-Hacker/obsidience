Buckingham Palace announced Prince William will attend the funeral of Norway's late King Harald V on Wednesday 9 September, representing King Charles; Princess Anne will also attend in a personal capacity. The 89-year-old monarch, who reigned 35 years, died last month. A procession will be followed by a service at Oslo Cathedral and a private burial at Akershus Fortress. New King Haakon VIII is expected to attend amid royal family controversies.
