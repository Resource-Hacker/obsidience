Buckingham Palace announced the Prince of Wales will attend the funeral of Norway's late King Harald V in Oslo on Wednesday 9 September, representing King Charles; Princess Anne will also go in a personal capacity. The king, who reigned 35 years, died last month aged 89. Large crowds are expected; a procession will be followed by a service at Oslo Cathedral and a private burial in the royal mausoleum at Akershus Fortress.
