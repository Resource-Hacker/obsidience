A 48-year-old man was arrested carrying explosives near the Weisweiler power plant in western Germany, suspected of sabotage attacks on electricity substations across three German states. Rockets were used to shoot wires across high-voltage cables causing short circuits. Police found 21 devices near power lines in Saxony alone. Interior Minister Armin Schuster warned the attacks could have led to a regional blackout.
