Kenya began enforcing a presidential order reserving small-scale hawking and petty retail businesses primarily for Kenyan citizens, with President Ruto telling foreign traders doing small businesses they should close them. Reactions were mixed; some Kenyans welcomed the move while others warned it could scare investors. A political economist called the policy populist ahead of upcoming elections, noting Kenya attracted a record 3.2 billion dollars in foreign direct investment in 2025.
