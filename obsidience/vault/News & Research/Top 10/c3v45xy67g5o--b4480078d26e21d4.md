At least two people were killed and 81 injured when "pyrotechnic material" detonated at a barracks in Viacha, Bolivia, though a health official told local television that 10 to 15 may have died. Fourteen people remain missing, including conscripts aged 18 and 19, and police warned of a further explosion as they searched the damaged rubble. The toll is preliminary and the higher figure is disputed.
