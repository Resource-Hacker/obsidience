At least two people were killed and 81 injured when pyrotechnic material detonated at a military barracks in Viacha, near La Paz, on Friday; fourteen remain missing, including a sergeant and 13 conscripts. Health official Rita Nebraska told local TV, per Reuters, the death toll could be 10-15. Police said dozens of homes were damaged and warned of further explosions; fireworks had been stored at the barracks and the cause is under investigation. President Rodrigo Paz ordered a thorough, technical and transparent investigation.
