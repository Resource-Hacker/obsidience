An explosion of "pyrotechnic material" at a Bolivian military barracks in Viacha killed at least two people and injured at least 81, with 14 missing. A health official told local television that 10-15 people may have died; the missing include a sergeant and 13 conscripts aged 18-19. Police said dozens of homes were damaged and warned of further explosions, urging people to stay 150m away. The President ordered a technical investigation. Reported 5 Sept 2026.
