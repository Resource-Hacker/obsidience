Reported 7 Sept 2026. China's finance ministry will provide 360 billion yuan ($53.6bn) to eight state-owned banks and insurance companies to shore up the financial system and support a slowing economy, Xinhua reported. The package targets three major lenders and five insurers, including ICBC and Agricultural Bank of China. It comes as Q2 GDP growth slowed to 4.3%, below Beijing's target, amid trade tensions, a property slump, and the Iran war's impact on oil.
