Reported 7 Sept 2026. After Sunday's preliminary results gave the AfD 43.8% and 39 of 83 seats in Saxony-Anhalt—three short of a majority—the party is seeking support to form a government, a first for a far-right party at state level since World War Two. Chancellor Merz called the result "seismic." The CDU's state share nearly halved to 17.2%. Talks are underway, possibly with the left-wing populist BSW.
