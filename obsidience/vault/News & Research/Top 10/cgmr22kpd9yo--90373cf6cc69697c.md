Gambia's President Adama Barrow urged calm after protests in Banjul over blackouts lasting up to 48 hours. Police used tear gas to disperse crowds as some chanted 'Barrow must go.' Barrow acknowledged shortcomings in the power system and promised a new 24-megawatt unit by the end of October. The unrest comes months before a December presidential election in which he seeks a controversial third term.
