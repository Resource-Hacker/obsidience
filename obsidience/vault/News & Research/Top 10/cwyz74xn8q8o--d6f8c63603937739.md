As Austria's new school year started, a law banning "traditional Muslim" head coverings for girls under 14 in public and private schools went into force. The government frames it as protecting young girls; the official Islamic Community opposes it and says it will support legal challenges. Repeated violations can lead to child-services notification and fines of up to 800 euros for guardians.

Uncertainty: critics question its constitutionality; a prior primary-school ban was struck down in 2020.
