Rescuers in Nepal are trying to reach dozens of workers trapped in hydropower tunnels nine days after the 26 August glacial flash floods on the Tibet border. Two men were pulled alive on Friday, one in critical condition; a third body was found. With 1,311 confirmed dead and 5,330 missing across Nepal and Tibet, more than 150 people are feared trapped in seven tunnels; officials believe another 39 are in the Trishuli 3A tunnel alone.
