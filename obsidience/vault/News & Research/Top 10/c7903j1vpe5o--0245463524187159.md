BBC reported that rescuers recovered two injured workers alive from Nepal's Trishuli 3A tunnel nine days after a devastating flash flood. A third person was found dead. Officials believed another 39 people remained in that tunnel, without knowing whether all were alive. Rescue efforts continued across seven hydropower tunnels, with engineers supplying air. A glacial collapse was suspected, but scientists were still examining the cause and considered climate change a possible factor. Report date: 4 September 2026.
