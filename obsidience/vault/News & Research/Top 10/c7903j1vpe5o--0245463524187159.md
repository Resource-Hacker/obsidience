Rescuers pulled two injured workers alive from the Trishuli 3A hydropower tunnel nine days after a glacial-triggered flash flood swept the Nepal-Tibet border; a third body was found in the same tunnel. Officials believe another 39 remain in that tunnel, and more than 150 are feared trapped across seven tunnels. Confirmed deaths in Nepal and Tibet stand at 1,311, with 5,330 missing. Uncertainty: unclear if all 39 are alive, cause still studied.
