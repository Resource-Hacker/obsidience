Hungary's new government under PM Peter Magyar established the National Asset Recovery Authority (NVVH), a "super institution" with policing and prosecutorial powers to recover funds allegedly diverted during Viktor Orban's rule. Magyar has cited around €60bn, though other estimates suggest three times that. Parliament appointed political scientist Anna Unger as its head. The authority will focus on the period from 2010 onward.
