Hungary's new government, led by PM Peter Magyar, has begun asset recovery via a National Asset Recovery Authority (NVVH) with broad policing and prosecutorial powers, targeting funds allegedly diverted during the Orban era from 2010. Magyar cites an estimate of around 60 billion euros (other estimates suggest more); the authority's new head, political scientist Anna Unger, said legally binding major verdicts within its first six years would be unrealistic.

Uncertainty: the 60 billion euro figure is Magyar's estimate; the authority's powers are broad but newly contested.
