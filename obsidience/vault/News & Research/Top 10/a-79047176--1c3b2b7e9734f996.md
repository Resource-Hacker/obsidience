Hungary's new government has established the National Asset Recovery Authority (NVVH) to recover funds and assets allegedly stolen or diverted under Viktor Orban's rule. New PM Peter Magyar has cited a sum of around €60 billion, though estimates suggest it could be triple that. The super-authority has both policing and prosecutorial powers. Political scientist Anna Unger was appointed to head the body, a choice that surprised some observers expecting a legal specialist.
