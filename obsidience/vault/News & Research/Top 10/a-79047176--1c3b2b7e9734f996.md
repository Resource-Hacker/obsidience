Hungary's new government under PM Peter Magyar established the National Asset Recovery Authority (NVVH), a super-institution with policing and prosecutorial powers, to recover funds it says were stolen or diverted under Viktor Orban. Magyar has cited around €60 billion, though other estimates suggest three times that. The body will focus on the Orban era from 2010. Parliament appointed political scientist Anna Unger to lead it, a decision that surprised observers.
