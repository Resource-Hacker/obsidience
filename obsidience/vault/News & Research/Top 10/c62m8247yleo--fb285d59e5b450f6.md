Médecins Sans Frontières warned that global funding cuts are collapsing Sudan's health system, with over 33 million people needing medical assistance. After the US closed USAID, dozens of organisations lost funding. In central Darfur, MSF saw admissions jump 22.5% year-on-year. In 2025, 1,620 people were killed in attacks on medical facilities—82% of all global deaths from such attacks. More than 19.5 million face crisis-level food insecurity.
