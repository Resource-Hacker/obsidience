Ukraine's chief prosecutor Ruslan Kravchenko resigned after anti-corruption investigators searched his offices, identifying a money-laundering scheme involving illegal call centres. He denied personal wrongdoing, calling the allegations "unsubstantiated." G7 ambassadors warned the scandal could endanger Ukraine's EU integration. Corruption scandals have plagued Kyiv throughout the war, with accusations at several members of President Zelensky's inner circle.
