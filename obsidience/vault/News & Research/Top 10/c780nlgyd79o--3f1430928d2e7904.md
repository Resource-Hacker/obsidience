Ukraine's chief prosecutor Ruslan Kravchenko resigned after anti-corruption investigators searched his offices and identified a money-laundering scheme involving call centres. Nabu and Sap said the network received money to protect scam call centres and "laundered millions in assets." Kravchenko denied wrongdoing, saying he did not want his office "used as a tool in political confrontation." G7 ambassadors warned the scandal could endanger Ukraine's EU integration.
