Ukraine's chief prosecutor Ruslan Kravchenko resigned after anti-corruption investigators searched his offices and identified a money-laundering scheme linked to call centres conducting phone scams. Nabu said the network received protection payments and used the funds to buy property and jewellery. Kravchenko denied wrongdoing, calling the allegations unsubstantiated. Zelensky had not yet commented publicly. G7 ambassadors warned the scandal could endanger Ukraine's EU integration prospects.
