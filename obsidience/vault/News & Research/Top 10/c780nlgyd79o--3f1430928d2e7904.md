Ukraine's chief prosecutor Ruslan Kravchenko resigned following a weekend search of his offices by anti-corruption investigators who identified a money-laundering scheme linked to illegal call centres in his office. Nabu said the network received money to protect scam call centres and "laundered millions in assets" used to buy property and jewellery. Kravchenko denied the allegations as "unsubstantiated" and said he did not want his office used as a "tool for political confrontation." G7 ambassadors warned the scandal could endanger Ukraine's EU integration. The resignation adds to a series of corruption cases that have plagued Kyiv throughout the war.
