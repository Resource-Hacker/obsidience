Houthi forces launched a ground offensive against the Saudi-led coalition in Yemen's southwest, seeking high ground and stronger control of the Bab el-Mandeb Strait. No official casualty figures were released; AFP tallied 129 deaths as of Friday, and shelling forced hundreds of families to flee coastal cities. Analysts said the Iran-backed Houthis, who control Sanaa, aimed to improve their negotiating position amid the wider Iran war and traffic restrictions in the Strait of Hormuz.
