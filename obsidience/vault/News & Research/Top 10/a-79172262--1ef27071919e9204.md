Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed could gain a stronger mandate if the country's planned constitutional reform merges the prime minister and presidential roles into a single executive office. Weeks of consultations with roughly 4,000 delegates recommended the change, though opposition parties boycotted the process. Abiy holds a massive parliamentary majority from June elections, making passage in parliament plausible. Critics question whether legal procedure equals popular legitimacy.
