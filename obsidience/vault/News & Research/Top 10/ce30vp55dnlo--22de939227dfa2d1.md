The UN General Assembly voted to replace the traditional Mercator world map with one that more accurately reflects Africa's size. The Togo-sponsored “Correct the Map” resolution passed with the support of 164 nations, including France and the United Kingdom; the US was the only country to vote against, with six abstentions. The vote is non-binding, but it is expected to change maps used by institutions worldwide and move away from a 16th-century projection that shrinks equatorial regions.
