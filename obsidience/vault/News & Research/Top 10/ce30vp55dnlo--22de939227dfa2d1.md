BBC reported that the UN General Assembly approved Togo's Correct the Map resolution with 164 countries in favour, the US opposing and six abstaining. The resolution is non-binding. Its supporters promote the Equal Earth projection, adopted by the African Union in February, as a more proportional representation of continental areas than Mercator maps. They argue that familiar map distortions influence perceptions of Africa. Report date: 4 September 2026 (updated 5 September 2026).
