The UN General Assembly voted to replace the traditional Mercator world map with one better reflecting Africa's size. The Togo-sponsored 'Correct the Map' resolution passed with 164 nations backing it, including France and the UK; the US was the only opponent, with six abstentions. The vote is non-binding, but institutions are expected to change maps; France's foreign ministry said it would stop using the Mercator projection.
