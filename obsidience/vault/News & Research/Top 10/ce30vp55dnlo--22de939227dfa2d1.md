The UN General Assembly passed the Togo-sponsored "Correct the Map" resolution 164 to 1, with the US the sole opponent and six abstentions, backing a projection that shows Africa's true size rather than the distorting Mercator map. The non-binding vote is expected to change the maps used by institutions worldwide and has become a symbolic fight over how Africa's place in the world is perceived.
