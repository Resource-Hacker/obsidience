The UN General Assembly voted 164 to adopt a Togo-sponsored "Correct the Map" resolution replacing the Mercator map with the Equal Earth projection, which better represents Africa's size; the vote is non-binding. The US was the only nation to vote against, with six abstentions; France and the UK supported it.
