Argentina announced it would file criminal charges against Navitas Petroleum for operating in seas off the Falkland Islands. The move follows President Milei's speech declaring the islands "Argentine, historically and legally." Navitas holds a 65% stake in the Sea Lion offshore project set to start production in 2028, containing an estimated 1.7 billion barrels of oil. The UK dismissed the charges and affirmed the 2013 referendum in which 99.8% voted to remain British.
