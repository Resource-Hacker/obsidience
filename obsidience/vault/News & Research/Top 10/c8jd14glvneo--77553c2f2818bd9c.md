Argentina will file criminal charges against Navitas Petroleum for operating in waters off the Falkland Islands, days after President Milei called drilling there a "clear and urgent danger" to Argentine sovereignty. Navitas holds a 65% stake in the Sea Lion project, set to produce from an estimated 1.7 billion barrels starting 2028. The UK dismissed the move, saying the islands' future is for Falkland Islanders to determine.
