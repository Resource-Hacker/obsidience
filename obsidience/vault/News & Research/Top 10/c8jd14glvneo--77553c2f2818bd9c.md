Argentina announced it would file criminal charges against oil company Navitas Petroleum for operating in waters off the Falkland Islands, days after President Javier Milei restated Argentina's sovereignty claim. Navitas holds a 65% stake in the Sea Lion project, set to begin production in 2028 from an estimated 1.7 billion barrels. Milei described drilling as a "clear and urgent danger" to Argentine sovereignty and dismissed the 2013 referendum in which 99.8% of islanders voted to remain British. The UK government said its position was "unwavering." Navitas said the remarks were "not expected to have a material effect" on the project.
