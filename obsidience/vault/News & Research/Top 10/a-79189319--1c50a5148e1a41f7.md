War criminal Ratko Mladic was buried with military honours in Belgrade, attended by Serbian and Bosnian Serb leaders. Bosnia's foreign minister withdrew almost all embassy staff from Belgrade, freezing diplomatic communication. The funeral is seen as political scoring ahead of Bosnia's October 4 election and Serbia's expected vote. Republika Srpska officials attended; Sarajevo called it an "anti-civilizational act" that threatens regional stability and EU accession prospects.
