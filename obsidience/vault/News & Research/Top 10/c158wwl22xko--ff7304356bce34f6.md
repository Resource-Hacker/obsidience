Kenya has opened a formal registration window for undocumented Burundians and other East Africans following President Ruto's directive targeting foreigners in small-scale businesses. Hundreds of Burundians scrambled to process travel documents after Ruto set Monday as the deadline. The government spokesperson said those undergoing registration would be presumed legally in Kenya. A 90-day grace period was granted for foreign nationals to regularise work permits and business registrations. A senior Kenyan official apologised to Burundians over harassment. Burundi's foreign minister warned that "if the hate speech continues things will certainly change." The UN reports around 16,000 Burundian refugees in Kenya.
