Thousands gathered in Belgrade for the funeral of convicted war criminal Ratko Mladić, who died aged 84 serving a life sentence for the 1995 Srebrenica genocide. Serbian ministers joined the crowds; the EU denounced the "glorification" of the convicted war criminal. The coffin was flown back on a government plane and carried by soldiers. The funeral comes days before President Vučić is expected to call an election.
