Thousands attended the funeral of Ratko Mladić in Belgrade, where he was buried with military honours after dying aged 84 in a UN prison. Mladić was convicted of genocide for his role in the 1995 Srebrenica massacre, in which around 8,000 Bosniak men and boys were killed. The EU denounced the "glorification" of the convicted war criminal. Several Serbian ministers joined the crowds. Free buses were organised from across Serbia and Bosnia.
