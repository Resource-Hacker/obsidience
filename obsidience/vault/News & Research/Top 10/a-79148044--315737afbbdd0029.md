Germany asked the International Court of Justice to dismiss a case brought by Nicaragua accusing Berlin of violating the 1948 Genocide Convention by supplying arms to Israel during its war with Hamas in Gaza. Germany argued the case falls outside the ICJ's jurisdiction and that its weapons export procedures are "more stringent than international regulations." Between November 2025 and March 2026, exports worth approximately €167 million were approved. A decision on Germany's dismissal bid is tentatively expected this year.
