Germany asked the International Court of Justice to dismiss Nicaragua's case accusing Berlin of violating the 1948 Genocide Convention by supplying arms to Israel in its war with Hamas. Germany argues the case is outside the court's jurisdiction and that its export controls are more stringent than international standards. Around €167 million in exports were approved between November 2025 and March 2026. Nicaragua is expected to present its arguments on Tuesday.
