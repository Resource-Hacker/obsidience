Germany asked the International Court of Justice to dismiss a case brought by Nicaragua accusing Berlin of violating the Genocide Convention by supplying arms to Israel. Germany argued the case falls outside ICJ jurisdiction and that its exports undergo stringent review. Between November 2025 and March 2026, exports worth around €167 million were approved. Nicaragua is expected to lay out its arguments Tuesday.
