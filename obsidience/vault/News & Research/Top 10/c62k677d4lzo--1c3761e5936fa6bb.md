President Trump signed an executive order to remove endangered-species protection for the grey wolf and the Mexican wolf, potentially allowing ranchers to kill them. The order gives Interior Secretary Doug Burgum 90 days to decide whether the populations have recovered enough to be delisted. Conservationists warn it risks decades of recovery work; FWS figures put Mexican wolves at 319 in 2025 and grey wolves at roughly 18,000. Reported 5 Sept 2026.
