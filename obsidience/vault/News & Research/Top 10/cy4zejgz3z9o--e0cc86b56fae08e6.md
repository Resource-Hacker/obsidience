Initial projections reported 6 Sept 2026 put the far-right AfD at 44.5% in the Saxony-Anhalt state election, far ahead of the CDU's 18.5%. The AfD says it has won a mandate to govern, but leaders will not know whether they hold a governing majority until it is clear if smaller parties clear the 5% threshold. A state win would be a first for a far-right party in Germany since World War Two, carrying powers over policing, local education and culture.
