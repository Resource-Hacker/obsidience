## Union Proposal to Tax Banks for Energy Bill Relief  
**Source**: BBC News — 10 Sep 2026 00:06:33 GMT  
**Citation**: [`source://ecb8cbc2-3c9f-48b1-8be4-d71ce6d266c1`](source://ecb8cbc2-3c9f-48b1-8be4-d71ce6d266c1)  

The TUC (Trade Union Congress) has proposed that a bank surcharge should be reversed, estimating the measure would raise **£9bn over four years** to reduce energy bills for some households.

The proposal was made by unions at the TUC annual conference in Burnham, Buckinghamshire.
