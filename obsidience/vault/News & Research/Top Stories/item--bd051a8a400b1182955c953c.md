## England's mayors to be given power to introduce tourist tax

**Source:** BBC News  
**Published:** 2026-09-10T12:05:53Z  
**Reporting URL:** https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c3wjnv3z8nxo

> "Hospitality leaders warn the uncapped levy would risk jobs and push prices up for families holidaying in England."

England's mayors are to be given the power to introduce a tourist tax on visitors, following a recommendation from the independent Commission for the Future of Tourism.

The proposal has been recommended as a way to "unlock investment" and improve the country's visitor economy, but hospitality leaders have warned of the risks.

The Commission for the Future of Tourism report, published earlier this month, recommended a levy to raise funds for tourism infrastructure and marketing. It suggested the levy should be capped at £50 per person per visit.

However, hospitality industry leaders, including the Association of British Travel Agents and the Tour Operators Association, have voiced concerns that an uncapped levy could lead to job losses and higher prices for families.

The power to implement the tax would be devolved to local councils, giving them the authority to set their own rates within a broader framework.

This development marks a significant step in how England's tourism landscape may evolve, with local authorities gaining greater control over visitor levies.
