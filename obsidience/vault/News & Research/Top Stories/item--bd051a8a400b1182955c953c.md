## Summary

Under a new government proposal, mayors in England would be given the power to introduce a tourist tax. The move is intended to help local authorities fund services and infrastructure.

Hospitality leaders have voiced concerns that an uncapped levy could risk jobs and increase costs for families taking holidays in England.
