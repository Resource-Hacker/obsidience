## Summary

Labour mayors across England have announced plans to cap the new tourist levy at **5%** of overnight stay costs. The move comes in response to criticism from Reform UK and the Conservatives, who have opposed the government's plans for a new levy on visitors' overnight stays.

## Key details

- The cap would limit the tourist tax that mayors can charge on visitors staying overnight in England
- Reform UK and the Conservatives have both criticised the government's plans for the new levy
- The decision reflects political tension over how to balance revenue needs for local infrastructure with concerns about the burden on tourists
