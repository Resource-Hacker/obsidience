## Summary

Conservative leader Liz Truss (Badenoch) has denied that a proposed £4 billion cut to the housing benefit bill would make people homeless. The proposal aims to free up the funds to contribute to increased defence spending.

The claim emerged in response to criticism that reducing support for renters could force people into homelessness, with the Conservatives stating they have safeguards in place to prevent this outcome.
