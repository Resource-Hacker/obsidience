## Finding: Gruffalo illustrator's concerns about AI images

**Source**: BBC News — [Gruffalo Granny illustrator says rise of AI images 'worrying'](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/videos/ce9ernkxx48o)
**Published**: 10 September 2026, 13:43:32 GMT

Axel Scheffler, the illustrator of the *Gruffalo* book series, told the BBC that the rise of AI-generated images is "worrying." He described AI pictures as "a breach of copyright" and noted they lose the charm of hand-drawn originals.

He was speaking alongside author Dame Julia Donaldson for the launch of their new book, *Gruffalo Granny*, in which Dame Julia reads an extract.

> "I think they're a breach of copyright," Scheffler told the BBC. "I can only hope that we have some common sense and the copyright laws are tightened."
