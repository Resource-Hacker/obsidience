## Summary

Seven people were arrested after four men were stabbed in south-east London, the Metropolitan Police confirmed.

One man remains in a critical condition in hospital; the other three are being treated for injuries not believed to be life-threatening.

## Details

Paramedics and the London Air Ambulance were called to Bromley High Street at about 14:20 BST on Thursday 10 September 2026.

A fight is believed to have taken place in or around KFC on Bromley High Street. The high street was closed both ways from Ringers Road to Ethelbert Road.

A witness, the manager of a nearby gambling shop, told police a fight "spilled out" onto the High Street. Police remain at the scene and said there is not believed to be any wider threat to the public.
