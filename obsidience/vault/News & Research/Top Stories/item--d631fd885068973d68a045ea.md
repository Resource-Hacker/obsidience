## Distilled Summary

Prince William and Catherine (the Princess of Wales) attended a launch event at Everton FC's Hill Dickinson stadium in Liverpool on 9 September 2026, unveiling the **On Your Side** campaign aimed at using sport's reach to reduce suicide stigma and encourage help-seeking.

The initiative promotes the **GOALS** framework:
- **G**et ready
- **O**pen the conversation
- **A**sk the question
- **L**isten and respond
- **S**upport and signpost

Prince William, expected to speak at the event, stated that "sport has the ability to reach people who may never walk through the door of a mental healthcare service," and emphasised the need for people to feel comfortable having "difficult conversations" with friends, family, or colleagues about mental health.

The project is supported by the FA, Premier League, Team GB, and the British Standards Institution, alongside mental health charities such as Chasing the Stigma (founder Jake Mills) and its "Hub of Hope" app.

A video features Harry Kane and Carl Frampton discussing stepping in to offer help. Prince William also narrates the film.

The couple visited a bench at the ground dedicated to **Gary Speed**, a former Everton player who died by suicide in 2011.

**Context:** Suicide is the leading cause of death for men under 50 in the UK, with approximately 7,000 lives lost annually.
