Selected news reporting from the BBC Top Stories Feed, distilled by Darwin and published by Alexandria beneath [News & Research](/News%20%26%20Research/News%20%26%20Research.md).

Each child Article preserves one complete cited distillation of a captured Feed item. BBC supplies the selection and ordering; this collection does not claim an independent ranking of world events. Reporting dates, attribution, uncertainty and immutable Source citations belong to each Article.

The Feed settings select how many publisher items to check, the collection interval, optional instructions for Darwin and the maximum number of active Feed Articles. This node inherits News & Research's Auto-curate policy. Eligible items proceed through [Distill](/Tasks/research/distill.md) and Alexandria's [Ingest](/Tasks/ingest.md) automatically.

The graph's child hierarchy lists the current Articles. Feed retention archives eligible older publications with their complete content and Sources, including earlier publications at the Feed's previous destination. The node has no fixed edition size or blanket expiry; the Feed's configured limit and each Article's OKF fields govern its lifecycle.
