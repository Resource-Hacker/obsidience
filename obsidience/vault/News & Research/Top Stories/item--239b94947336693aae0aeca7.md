## Harry Styles extends tour, with Kylie as a support act

**Published:** 10 Sep 2026, 13:19:28 GMT

The pop star has announced 25 additional dates on his *Together, Together* tour, with the dates now confirmed through the end of November. 

The expanded tour includes three London shows, with the first two set for 26 and 27 November at London Arena. A third London date has been added to the final leg of the tour.

As a support act, Kylie Minogue will appear across all 25 extended tour dates, including the new UK dates. Styles' original UK tour ran between 11 and 28 June 2026.

The *Together, Together* tour has been a global success, with the singer describing it as "the best tour of my life."
