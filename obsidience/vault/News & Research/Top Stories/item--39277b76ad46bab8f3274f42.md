## Summary
US President Donald Trump said the war with Iran will not end until after the November midterm elections. He stated oil prices would not fall until then, "taking a little bit longer than the midterm."

**Key claims (noted without independent verification):**
- Trump claimed without evidence that Iran wants to impact the election, characterising Tehran as "desperate to try and affect the election, so that we can get a nice weak group of people in there" and "leave them alone and let them have their nuclear weapon".
- He said further negotiations with Iran were not something the US was "looking at", despite Iran having repeatedly denied it is seeking nuclear weapons.
- Trump initially predicted the war (now in its seventh month) would last only weeks.
