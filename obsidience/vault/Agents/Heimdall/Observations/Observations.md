title: Observations
---

Heimdall's working observations preserve review findings, validation failures, unresolved risks, and the next verification.
They inherit the owner-selected Auto-curate permission from
[Heimdall](/Agents/Heimdall/Heimdall.md); a child can explicitly disable it.
Permission does not make an inference verified or grant new Tools.

[Temporary Observations](/Agents/Heimdall/Observations/Temporary%20Observations/Temporary%20Observations.md)
holds bounded, unverified working summaries. Useful durable findings belong in
ordinary, evidence-linked subject Articles. Cross-agent publication still belongs
to [Alexandria](/Agents/Alexandria/Alexandria.md) and follows the destination's policy.
