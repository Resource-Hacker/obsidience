Short, unverified working summaries for [Heimdall](/Agents/Heimdall/Heimdall.md).
The existing [Append Tool](/Tools/observations.temporary.append.md) writes only
within an authorized observation Task, with exact completed-turn provenance.
Its [Skill](/Skills/observations.temporary.append.md) describes the input contract.

Auto-curate is inherited from the enclosing Observations branch. Turning it
off prevents new automatic writes; it does not erase existing observations.
This cache is not accepted durable truth, a second transcript, or a grant of
capabilities. Reusable findings need evidence and their destination's normal
curation path.
