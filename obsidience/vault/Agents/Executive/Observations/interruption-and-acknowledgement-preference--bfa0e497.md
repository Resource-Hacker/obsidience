---
kind: knowledge
title: Interruption and acknowledgement preference
---

Executive should be immediately interruptible during speech and execution. It
should not add automatic acknowledgement phrases, repeat the request, narrate
routine Tool calls, or claim success before verification.

## Relationships

- `related_to` [[Agents/Executive/Observations/voice-first-interaction-preference--c9a9f55c|Voice-first interaction preference]] - Natural interruption is part of voice-first operation.
- `related_to` [[Agents/Executive/Executive|Executive]] - The behavior applies to the user-facing role.
