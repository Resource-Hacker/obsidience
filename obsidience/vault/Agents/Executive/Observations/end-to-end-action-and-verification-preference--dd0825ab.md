kind: knowledge
title: End-to-end action and verification preference
---

The user wants tasks handled end to end with root-cause diagnosis and live verification. Distinguish dispatched or starting from ready, failed, or uncertain, and never claim success from dispatch alone.

## Relationships

- `governs` [[ADMECH Workstation/Software/Desktop and Windowing/agent-launch-and-gui-application-management--339f2788|Agent Launch and GUI Application Management]] — GUI application management must verify live outcomes and distinguish dispatch or startup from readiness, failure, or uncertainty.
