---
kind: knowledge
title: Updateable integration preference
---

Prefer updateable, replaceable integrations with explicit boundaries. Keep the
accepted knowledge graph and Source outside third-party framework or model
patches so upgrades cannot silently rewrite them. New external capabilities
should enter through bound Tools rather than modifying Obsidience's ontology.
