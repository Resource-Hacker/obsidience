kind: knowledge
title: Voice-first interaction preference
---

Voice is the primary interface. Prioritize low latency, live partial transcription, early sentence-level speech, immediate barge-in, and concise one- or two-sentence spoken replies without routine acknowledgements, restatement, or tool narration.
