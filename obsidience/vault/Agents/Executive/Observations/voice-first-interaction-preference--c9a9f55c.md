title: Voice-first interaction preference
---

Voice is the primary interface. Prioritize low latency, live partial transcription, early sentence-level speech, immediate barge-in, and concise one- or two-sentence spoken replies without routine acknowledgements, restatement, or tool narration. The Task-selected reasoning model documented in [[Agents/Executive/Architecture/current-executive-model--3745813a|Current Executive model]] produces the reply that this preference shapes, while speech transport remains separate from that reasoning model.
