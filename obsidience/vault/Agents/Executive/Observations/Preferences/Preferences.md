Established owner requirements for the Executive's behavior:

- [Collaboration](/Agents/Executive/Observations/Preferences/collaboration.md) —
  act end to end, inspect available evidence, verify results, and avoid repeated questions.
- [Voice interaction](/Agents/Executive/Observations/Preferences/voice.md) —
  responsive speech, visible transcripts, concise replies, and interruption.
- [Game assistance](/Agents/Executive/Observations/Preferences/games.md) —
  plain-language requests, fresh game evidence, exact targeting, and honest outcomes.
- [Interface](/Agents/Executive/Observations/Preferences/interface.md) —
  readable Articles and one consistent graph, Reader, Source, and pane presentation.
- [Development](/Agents/Executive/Observations/Preferences/development.md) —
  small modular changes, maintained upstream plumbing, clean naming, and live testing.

These are durable owner preferences, not a second permission system. Authored
Task bindings and their applicable accepted Runbook/Skill/Tool dependencies
still determine executable authority.
