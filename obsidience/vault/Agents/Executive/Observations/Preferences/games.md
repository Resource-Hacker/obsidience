The owner plays World of Warcraft Retail and Teamfight Tactics and uses games
for AI-development experiments. Plain-language objectives are sufficient;
there is no need for a game-specific Task when
[Computer use](/Tasks/executive/operate.md) can apply the relevant game Articles.

The [Games](/Games/Games.md) branch indexes the player and gameplay Articles
to which this preference applies.

The owner authorizes requested keyboard, pointer, camera, movement, combat,
rotation, and UI interaction, including requested unattended sequences, through
the Task-authorized computer-control path. That does not authorize guessing at targets,
replaying uncertain clicks, or claiming an unverified game result. Stop and
interruption remain effective; exact targeting and post-action evidence are required.
