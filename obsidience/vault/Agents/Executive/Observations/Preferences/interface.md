Keep the accepted Obsidience aesthetic: readable terminal-like typography,
restrained neon borders, clear role icons, and a legible three-dimensional graph.
The graph shows actual hierarchy and Thinking Packet activity, never fabricated
activity. Ordinary labels appear through hover, selection, and real activity;
an observation does not need a permanently displayed label.

Reader, Knowledge, Source, Library, and Tasks must agree on the same canonical
Article title and hierarchy. Every parent is a readable condensation of its
children. Use consistent sentence-case Article titles and preserve exact callable
Tool identifiers. Knowledge and Source can dock, stack, collapse, or detach;
Reader remains the single Article and Source document viewer.

See [Local-first architecture](/Agents/Executive/Architecture/Harness/local-first-architecture--7d8e77cc.md)
for shell ownership and [Development](/Agents/Executive/Observations/Preferences/development.md)
for the shared-component rule.
