title: Temporary Observations
---

Each child Article is one bounded, unverified observation or cumulative
conversation summary. A summary preserves the goal, constraints and corrections,
reported results with their evidence status, and outstanding work. It is not proof
that an action succeeded.

[Compact](/Tasks/observations/immediate/compact.md) creates conversation summaries.
At a conversation boundary, [Promote](/Tasks/observations/durable/promote.md) assigns
Alexandria the exact committed bundle: archive it in Source, then propose only
justified durable changes under their proper subjects. Review decides acceptance.

The active conversation's latest committed summary survives ordinary expiry;
pending promotion inputs remain available until archived. Other entries follow
the bounded cache policy in [Observation lifecycle](/Agents/Executive/Architecture/observations.md).
