The user wants plain-language game requests to be sufficient. Asking Executive to
finish the remaining World of Warcraft talents should select only legal,
unfilled choices from fresh game evidence and verify the resulting state.
Asking Executive to play the next best Teamfight Tactics character, card, or round
should use the current board and the accepted next-best-play policy rather than
generic advice.

These requests grant the requested action, not permission to guess. Obsidience
may execute them only after the required observation and computer-control Tools
are bound, checked out, freshly targeted, and independently verified. An
unqualified request to open or launch World of Warcraft follows [[ADMECH Workstation/Software/Games/World of Warcraft/world-of-warcraft-launch-policy--3e5e73cc|World of Warcraft launch policy]] for the registered application, launch chain, and readiness verification.
