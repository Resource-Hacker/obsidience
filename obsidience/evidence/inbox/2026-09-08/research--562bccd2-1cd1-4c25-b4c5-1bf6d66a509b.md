## 10. Three years on, Morocco earthquake victims still await full reconstruction

Three years after the September 2023 earthquake killed over 2,900 people in Morocco's Al Haouz region, some survivors remain in tents. The government has built or rehabilitated 54,425 homes and distributed over 7.2 billion dirhams in aid. However, more than 3,000 families report receiving no state help, often due to residency documentation issues. In some villages 90% is rebuilt; in others, half the families still wait.
