Canada's retaliatory tariffs on nearly C$28 billion worth of American products took effect Tuesday, with rates up to 50% covering steel, furniture, and cotton. At least 27 people have been killed in southern Lebanon over three days.

Ukrainian investigators say it serves as a covert military logistics corridor for Russia's war in Ukraine. South Korean estimates put North Korean weapons transfers at $7–14 billion, with roughly 11,000 North Korean troops fighting in Russia. Construction took 495 days.

The far-right AfD won 43.8% of the vote in Saxony-Anhalt, falling three seats short of a majority. Its deputy leader called on opponents to negotiate, saying "democracy demands" it. The AfD is assessed as right-wing extremist by domestic intelligence.

## 8. Nepal observes national day of mourning for flash flood victims

Nepal marked a national day of mourning with flags at half-mast as rescue teams continue searching for survivors of a glacial flash flood. At least 1,287 people are confirmed dead and over 5,000 remain missing. Rescuers are intensifying operations in hydropower tunnels where hundreds of workers are believed trapped. On the Tibetan side, 31 people died with 531 unaccounted for. A 64-year-old woman was rescued more than 10 days after being buried.
