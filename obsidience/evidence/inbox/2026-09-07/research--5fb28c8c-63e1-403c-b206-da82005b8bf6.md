## 4. Indonesia volcano eruption strands 270,000
Mount Anak Krakatau, 150km from Jakarta, began erupting late Friday, sending volcanic ash into the capital and grounding more than 2,000 flights. The Geological Agency warned the probability of further eruptions "remains high". No tsunami warning was issued. Reported 7 September 2026.

## 8. Nepal observes national day of mourning for flood victims

At least 1,287 people are known to have died and 5,083 remain missing in Nepal; Chinese media reported 31 dead and 531 unaccounted for in Tibet.
