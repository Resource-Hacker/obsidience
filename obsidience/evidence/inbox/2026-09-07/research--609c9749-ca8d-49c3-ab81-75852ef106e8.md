## 1. Germany's far-right AfD wins big in Saxony-Anhalt, falls short of outright majority

Interim results of the 6 September state election project the far-right AfD at a record 44% of the vote, far ahead of the CDU's 17%, and 39 of 83 state seats, three short of the 42 needed for a majority. The party claims a governing mandate; a state government would be the first far-right one in Germany since World War Two. Domestic intelligence assesses the party as right-wing extremist.

## 2. Five dead after Amazon cargo plane crashes at Miami airport

An Amazon Air Boeing 767-300, operated by charter company 21 Air, overshot the runway while landing at Miami International on Sunday, struck vehicles, and caught fire. Miami-Dade officials say five people are dead and five seriously injured, three critically; the FAA grounded flights while crews responded. The NTSB has not yet determined a cause.

Reported: 7 September 2026. Direct article: https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c0e3p1j7n3ro · source://4bec28e1-185c-4e1f-bb63-1c739428dcbd

## 3. Zelensky expects war to continue into winter after US envoy talks

US envoys Witkoff and Kushner met Putin in Moscow, then Zelensky in Kyiv on Sunday; no major breakthrough was announced. Zelensky says the war "looks like" it will continue into winter and is requesting air-defence and energy support, while the sides agreed officials from Ukraine, Europe, and the US will keep meeting. Aerial strikes continued over the weekend.

Reported: 6 September 2026. Direct article: https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6248nvqy8po · source://81ddcc8e-2777-405d-a856-a9eb2caba24a

## 4. Volcanic ash from Anak Krakatau strands around 170,000 passengers in Indonesia

Eruptions of Anak Krakatau from Friday night sent ash over airspaces, and authorities temporarily closed eight airports, including Jakarta's Soekarno-Hatta. The transport ministry said 1,558 flights were delayed and roughly 170,000 passengers stranded. BMKG reported ash reached 6,000 m on the Java side and 15,000 m on the Sumatra side; no tsunami warning was issued.

## 5. Iran warns of "faster, heavier, more painful response" to US strikes

Iran's top negotiator Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf warned on Sunday of "a faster, heavier, and more painful response" a day after Washington said it struck Iranian oil tankers. Iranian state media claimed the navy attacked a US remote-controlled vessel in the Strait of Hormuz; the US denied the claim. The strait, crucial to global oil, has been effectively closed since the war began in February.

## 6. Dozens feared trapped after student hostel collapses in Delhi

A five-storey student hostel in Delhi's Satya Niketan area collapsed around 13:30 local time Sunday, with as many as 50 people feared inside. Local police, via PTI, reported at least three dead, three critical, and at least eight rescued; an eyewitness said a neighbouring hostel also fell. Basement construction was underway, and police are searching for the building's owner.

Reported: 6 September 2026. Direct article: https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c8r6ezx1jmro · source://5d891ec2-6c3d-4c3d-a999-90d13e8ebcc0

## 7. UN votes to adopt new world map to reflect Africa's true size

The UN General Assembly voted 164 to adopt a Togo-sponsored "Correct the Map" resolution replacing the Mercator map with the Equal Earth projection, which better represents Africa's size; the vote is non-binding. The US was the only nation to vote against, with six abstentions; France and the UK supported it. Critics say Mercator long distorted perceptions of equatorial regions.

Reported: 4 September 2026 (updated 5 September 2026). Direct article: https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ce30vp55dnlo · source://255c5f6a-a110-4517-a8a7-2b0a53fd3fe8

## 8. Hungary begins hunt for Orban-era billions through new asset-recovery authority

Hungary's new government, led by PM Peter Magyar, has begun asset recovery via a National Asset Recovery Authority with broad policing and prosecutorial powers, targeting funds allegedly diverted during the Orban era from 2010. Magyar cites an estimate of around 60 billion euros; the authority's new head, political scientist Anna Unger, said legally binding major verdicts within its first six years would be unrealistic.

Reported: 6 September 2026 (Deutsche Welle). Direct article: https://www.dw.com/en/hungary-begins-hunt-for-corrupt-regime-s-billions/a-79047176 · source://9e52f2c7-267b-4693-b487-33864a5bc6c0

## 9. At least two dead in blast at Bolivia military barracks

A "pyrotechnic material" explosion at a military barracks near La Paz on Friday killed at least two people and injured 81, per the defence minister, with 14 still missing; a health official told local television that 10 to 15 people may have died. Police said dozens of homes were damaged, and the cause remains under investigation.

Reported: 5 September 2026. Direct article: https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c3v45xy67g5o · source://0bf03021-e08b-4a4e-aca3-7410a62ff097

## 10. Austria's headscarf ban for under-14s takes effect as school year begins

As Austria's new school year started, a law banning "traditional Muslim" head coverings for girls under 14 in public and private schools went into force. The government frames it as protecting young girls; the official Islamic Community opposes it and says it will support legal challenges. Repeated violations can lead to child-services notification and fines of up to 800 euros for guardians.
