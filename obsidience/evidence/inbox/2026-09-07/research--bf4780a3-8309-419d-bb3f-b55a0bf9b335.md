## 1. Kyiv at record high for air raid alerts as Russia strikes round the clock

Kyiv has suffered more daily air raid alerts than at any time since Russia's 2022 invasion, BBC Verify found, averaging about 10 a day since late August as strikes shifted to daytime. Alerts have disrupted schools, offices, and transit. Ukraine's air force says Russia launched 2,800 fast jet-powered drones in August; Kyiv is developing interceptors, and analysts warn the campaign targets Ukraine's economy ahead of winter.

## 2. US envoys hold Ukraine talks after Moscow meeting, no breakthrough

US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner held their first official visit to Kyiv, meeting President Zelenskyy in three rounds of talks after talks with Vladimir Putin in Moscow, where a declared three-day ceasefire covered the meeting. DW reports the push produced no breakthrough, with sides still divided over territorial concessions. Zelenskyy said Ukraine needs a winter military-aid package; the envoys called the talks encouraging.

## 3. Israeli strikes in southern Lebanon kill 12

Lebanon's health ministry says Israeli strikes on the village of Kfar Roummane killed at least 12 people, including a paramedic, in an overnight attack on a residential building and a second on a vehicle. The Israeli military says it hit Hezbollah infrastructure in retaliation for drone attacks. President Joseph Aoun warns the dangerous escalation is jeopardising the US-brokered ceasefire. Hezbollah rejected the disarmament-withdrawal framework, and residents are again fleeing.

Reported 7 September 2026. https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c5yl96vz0edo — source://4a03362d-8052-48eb-b58b-cc431e7ab425

## 4. Germany's far-right AfD seeks first state power after Saxony-Anhalt win

Preliminary results show the AfD won 43.8% of the vote in Saxony-Anhalt and is three seats short of a majority, prompting the party to call on other parties to work with it. Chancellor Friedrich Merz said he was deeply shocked; no far-right party has governed a German state since World War Two. The party is assessed as right-wing extremist by domestic intelligence, and talks could run for weeks.

Reported 7 September 2026. https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cpd0113082eo — source://f229caeb-13a7-4622-83be-ce9c7d718cf3

## 5. Thousands attend funeral of convicted war criminal Ratko Mladić in Belgrade

Thousands, including Serbian ministers, attended the funeral of Ratko Mladić, the Bosnian Serb general convicted of genocide over the 1995 Srebrenica massacre, who died at 84 in The Hague. The EU denounced his glorification, saying war-criminal revisionism contradicts European values. Mladić remains a hero to many Serbs, and the event occurred days before President Vucic is expected to call an election.

Reported 7 September 2026. https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cn5dkk7yw74o — source://b8e8a898-9b1a-40cc-96ea-e26ceaa28d8f

Hungary's new Prime Minister Peter Magyar promised during his election campaign to recover government funds stolen or diverted by the Orbán administration. DW reports a new "super authority" is about to begin that asset-recovery work, and its success or failure will have a big impact. The effort marks a test of the new government's ability to reverse spending from the previous administration.

## 7. Nicaragua challenges German arms exports to Israel at ICJ

At ICJ hearings in The Hague, Nicaragua accused Germany of violating the 1948 Genocide Convention by supplying arms to Israel amid its war with Hamas in Gaza; Germany asked the court to dismiss the case on jurisdiction grounds. Germany says its exports are strictly reviewed, and a full trial would likely take years, possibly contingent on the separate South Africa genocide case.

## 8. Indonesia volcano eruption leaves 340,000 stranded as airports extend closure

Anak Krakatau, 150km from Jakarta, erupted late Friday, sending ash over the capital and forcing a second day of airport closures that stranded more than 340,000 passengers and disrupted nearly 3,000 flights. Jakarta's main airport will not reopen until Monday night. Indonesia's Geological Agency warned the probability of further eruptions remains high; authorities set a 3km exclusion zone and said there is no tsunami warning.

Reported 7 September 2026. https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/czezydp4l97o — source://87080e07-a202-4ee4-8e1b-b9aa158187ee

## 9. Five dead after Amazon cargo plane overruns Miami runway

Five people were killed and five seriously injured when an Amazon cargo plane operated by 21 Air overshot the runway at Miami International Airport on Sunday and hit vehicles beyond it. NTSB investigators began work on the cause; it is not yet clear whether the dead were aboard or on the ground. The crash grounded the airport briefly during the busy Labor Day travel weekend.

Reported 7 September 2026. https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c0e3p1j7n3ro — source://46df5a8a-bbfb-40f9-93b5-2362597cfbfc

## 10. China to pump $54bn into state banks and insurers

China's finance ministry will inject 360 billion yuan ($53.6bn) into eight state banks and insurers, Xinhua said, to strengthen their risk resistance and support the real economy. The move aims to shore up the world's second-largest economy amid trade tensions, the Iran war's oil-price impact, a property slump, and slowing growth; second-quarter GDP rose 4.3%, below Beijing's target range of 4.5%–5%.
