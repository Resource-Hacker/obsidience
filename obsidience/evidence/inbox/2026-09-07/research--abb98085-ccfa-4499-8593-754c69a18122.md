## 1. Indonesia volcano eruption leaves 340,000 stranded as airports extend closure
Anak Krakatau, 150 km from Jakarta, erupted late Friday 5 September, sending ash up to 15,000 m over Sumatra and 6,000 m over Java. The eruption disrupted nearly 3,000 flights and stranded over 340,000 passengers. Soekarno-Hatta and Halim Perdanakusuma airports extended closures until Monday night as ash lingered in airspace, with a 3 km exclusion zone enforced around the crater. The Geological Agency warned further eruptions remained likely.

## 2. Flight recorders recovered from 'devastating' Amazon cargo plane crash
NTSB investigators recovered the flight recorders from the Amazon cargo plane that crashed at Miami International Airport Sunday 6 September. The Boeing 767-300, operated by 21 Air from San Juan, Puerto Rico, overshot the runway shortly before 14:00 local time, killing five people and seriously injuring five when it struck two vehicles. NTSB chair Jennifer Homendy said recovery of victims was the priority and the cause was still under investigation.
Report date: 7 September 2026. URL: https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ce8e32n8epeo. Citation: source://55605726-4a5a-4ffe-815a-427e3f974117.

## 3. Kyiv air raid alerts at record high as Russia launches round-the-clock strikes
BBC Verify analysis of the Kyiv Digital app found Kyiv suffered more daily air-raid alerts than at any time since Russia's full-scale invasion began in 2022. Sirens have sounded on average ten times a day since late August, with 76 alerts in the seven days from 27 August. Ukrainian air-force spokesman Yurii Ihnat said Russia launched 2,800 fast jet-powered drones in August, nearly double July. Analysts say the strikes aim to hurt morale and the economy.
Report date: 7 September 2026. URL: https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cjdvx9pz44ro. Citation: source://ed372241-f107-436e-9feb-18a877c530e4.

## 4. Israeli strikes in southern Lebanese village kill 12
The Lebanese health ministry said Israeli air strikes on the village of Kfar Roummane overnight killed at least twelve people, including two children and four women, when a residential building was destroyed. A second strike on a paramedic vehicle killed one more. The IDF said it hit Hezbollah infrastructure in retaliation for a drone attack on troops. President Joseph Aoun said the escalation was jeopardising the June US-brokered ceasefire, which Lebanon's health ministry reported has left at least 4,362 dead in total.
Report date: 7 September 2026. URL: https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c5yl96vz0edo. Citation: source://b8d30574-0c83-4715-b9ec-be526510c477.

## 5. Thousands turn out in Serbia for funeral of 'Butcher of Bosnia' Ratko Mladić
Thousands attended the funeral in Belgrade of Ratko Mladić, who died aged 84 in a UN prison in The Hague serving a life sentence for the 1995 Srebrenica genocide. The coffin was flown home on a government plane and guarded by soldiers, with ministers among the mourners. The EU denounced the "glorification", and Serbian Orthodox Patriarch Porfirije led the service. Victim families in Sarajevo said Mladić should be remembered only as a butcher, not a hero.
Report date: 7 September 2026. URL: https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cn5dkk7yw74o. Citation: source://a9ba7ae6-7299-4a9a-8167-4048a9066bf9.

## 6. Germany's AfD says 'democracy demands' parties work with them after Saxony-Anhalt win
Preliminary results from Sunday's Saxony-Anhalt election show the far-right AfD on 43.8% of the vote and 39 of 83 seats, three short of an outright majority. Party figures are seeking support from other groups; deputy leader Beatrix von Storch said "democracy demands" talks. Chancellor Friedrich Merz described the result as "seismic" and the CDU's worst state defeat in decades, as the party's vote almost halved to 17.2%. No far-right party has governed a German state since World War Two.
Report date: 7 September 2026. URL: https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cpd0113082eo. Citation: source://9e74c634-4a6e-4c4d-86d6-29bb3039f2aa.

## 7. Grindr to pay £26m to settle claims it allegedly shared users' HIV status
Grindr agreed on 2 September to pay £26m, in two £13m tranches, to settle a class action alleging it shared users' personal information, including HIV status, with data analytics firms Apptimize and Localytics before 2020. The firm disputes the allegations and says the settlement includes no admission of liability. The lawsuit was filed in the UK in 2024 and later served in the US, with more than 11,000 claimants. Grindr said it overhauled privacy practices after 2020.
Report date: 7 September 2026. URL: https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cn8e63qdqwzo. Citation: source://1f2752c8-2ace-44ac-a363-e00b7f28d7fc.

## 8. Nepal observes national day of mourning for victims of flash flood disaster
Nepalis observed a national day of mourning on Monday, the last day of the 13-day Hindu mourning period, for victims of a glacial-collapse flash flood along the Nepal–Tibet border. At least 1,287 people are known to have died and more than 5,000 remain missing in Nepal; on the Tibetan side, 31 are dead and 531 unaccounted for. Rescue teams continue ground, air, and tunnel searches, and a 64-year-old woman was pulled alive from a mud-buried house on Saturday more than ten days after the wall of ice, rock, and mud hit her village.
Report date: 7 September 2026. URL: https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c2kw1qek7gdo. Citation: source://85e33872-46ae-4573-ba35-07a1f25f3667.

## 9. China to pump $54bn into state banks and insurers to boost economy
China's finance ministry will inject 360 billion yuan (about $53.6bn) into eight state-owned banks and insurers, led by the Industrial and Commercial Bank of China and the Agricultural Bank of China, state news agency Xinhua said. Beijing says the package will strengthen lenders' ability to serve the real economy and withstand external shocks. The move comes as China's GDP growth slowed to 4.3% in the second quarter, below the government's 4.5%-5% annual target.
Report date: 7 September 2026. URL: https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cx2z37qj4wno. Citation: source://367f37f1-09b5-408e-a543-65746039650a.

## 10. Peace talks rich in symbolism but fundamental differences between Russia and Ukraine remain
US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner held symbolic talks with President Vladimir Putin in Moscow and later met President Volodymyr Zelensky in Kyiv, seeking to revive efforts to end the war. Despite the diplomacy, the latest push produced no breakthrough, and the expectation in Kyiv is that the fighting will continue. The visits came as Kyiv endures its heaviest air-raid season of the war and both sides remain separated on core territorial and security issues.
