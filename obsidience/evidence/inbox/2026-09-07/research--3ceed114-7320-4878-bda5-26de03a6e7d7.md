## 3. Volcanic eruption closes eight Indonesian airports, stranding about 170,000 passengers

Eruptions of Mount Anak Krakatau, beginning late Friday, sent ash over airspace near Jakarta and forced temporary closure of eight Indonesian airports, stranding around 170,000 passengers. The transport ministry said 1,558 flights were delayed, mainly affecting routes to Singapore, Doha, Sydney and Kuala Lumpur, with operations suspended until 09:00 local Monday. Authorities said there was no tsunami warning; residents were urged to wear masks against ash. Basement excavation work had been underway, and witnesses said trapped students called from under the rubble.
