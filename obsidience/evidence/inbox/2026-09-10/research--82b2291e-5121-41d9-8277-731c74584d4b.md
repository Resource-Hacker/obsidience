## Four hurt and seven arrested in London stabbing

**Summary.** Seven people have been arrested after four men were stabbed in south-east London, the Metropolitan Police said on 10 September 2026. One man remains in a critical condition; the other three are being treated for injuries not believed to be life-threatening.

**Where.** Paramedics and the London Air Ambulance were called to Bromley High Street at about 14:20 BST on Thursday. Bromley High Street was closed both ways from Ringers Road to Ethelbert Road. Police remained at the scene.

**What happened.** Police believe a fight took place in or around a KFC restaurant. One witness, the manager of Merkur Slots gambling shop, said she saw "a fight spill out" on to the High Street and locked three customers inside the premises while waiting for the situation to calm.

**Wider threat.** Police say there is not believed to be any wider threat to the public.
