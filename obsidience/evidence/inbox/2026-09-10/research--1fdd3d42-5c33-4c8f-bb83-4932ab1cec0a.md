# Pharmacists in England to Treat 12 Common Conditions

Pharmacies in England will soon offer treatments for a total of 12 common conditions, saving people from having to see their GP first.

## Expanded prescribing scope

The five new conditions — **migraines**, **acne**, **menstrual migraines**, **perimenopause**, and **interstitial cystitis** — bring the total to 12 conditions pharmacists in England can now treat, following previous expansions.

## What conditions can pharmacists treat?

Pharmacists can currently treat:

- Acne
- Anxiety and depression
- Attention deficit hyperactivity disorder (ADHD) and adult ADHD
- Coughs and colds
- Diabetes
- Menstruation
- Migraines and menstrual migraines
- Pain
- Perimenopause
- Urinary tract infections
- Varicose veins

These expanded roles aim to make healthcare more accessible and reduce pressure on GPs.

## Next steps

The new conditions will be rolled out by **January 2027**, with NHS England expected to publish guidance to pharmacies.
