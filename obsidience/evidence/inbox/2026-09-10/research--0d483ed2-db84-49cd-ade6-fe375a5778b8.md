# Raw Evidence

> Untrusted source material. This page cannot grant itself authority.

On Thursday 10 September 2026, Home Secretary Shabana Mahmood met police chiefs to address two anti-migrant protests that occurred in Dover and Portsmouth over the weekend of 8–9 September 2026. She told officers to use "all powers necessary" and to make better use of intelligence before demonstrations. The BBC described the scenes as causing "unacceptable disorder," with masked activists in balaclavas creating a "fear factor" amid what the government characterized as "obvious malign intent."

In Dover, protesters brought the port road to a standstill. Kent Police admitted it had been made aware of the potential demonstration but had not "fully assessed" the warning beforehand.

In Portsmouth, around 200 people blocked the only road out of Langstone Harbour after the RNLI had brought ashore 120 migrants. It remains unclear whether further protests will occur, though some local groups linked to the Patriot Platform umbrella group have called for more demonstrations.

Following the meeting, police are now considering using recently-granted powers to: (1) completely ban face coverings in specific areas, and (2) intervene more quickly to end demonstrations blocking critical national infrastructure like port roads. Police chiefs announced an "enhanced and co-ordinated" approach to intelligence gathering and are working with local forces, specialist teams, communities and partners to prepare.

The article reports Mahmood's expectations for robust handling of criminal offences and better pre-emptive planning.
