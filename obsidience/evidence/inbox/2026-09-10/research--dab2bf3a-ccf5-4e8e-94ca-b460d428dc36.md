# Bromley High Street Stabbing: Four Hospitalised

Four people were taken to hospital following a stabbing in Bromley High Street, London, on Thursday, 10 September 2026.

- **Time:** 14:19 BST on Thursday, 10 September 2026
- **Location:** Bromley High Street, London
- **Emergency response:** London Ambulance Service and London Air Ambulance crews attended the scene
- **Road closure:** The high street was closed both ways from Ringers Road to Ethelbert Road
- **Police presence:** Part of the high street was cordoned off by police with emergency vehicles at the scene
