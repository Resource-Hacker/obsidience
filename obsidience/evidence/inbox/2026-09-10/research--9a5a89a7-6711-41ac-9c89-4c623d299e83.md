## Report

**Date:** 10 September 2026, 15:38 GMT  
**Source:** BBC News  
**Reporting URL:** https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c4g7vr0lngwo

Yemen's Houthi movement reportedly seized the strategic port city of **Mokha** on the Red Sea coast. The capture would bring the Iran-backed group closer to controlling the **Bab al-Mandab Strait**, a narrow and key shipping route linking the Red Sea and the Gulf of Aden.

Mokha is a major port on Yemen's western coast, historically a hub for trade. Control of Mokha would extend the Houthis' existing maritime reach and pressure the strait, through which roughly 10% of global trade passes.
