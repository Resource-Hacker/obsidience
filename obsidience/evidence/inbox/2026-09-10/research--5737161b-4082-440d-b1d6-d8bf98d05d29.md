# Four hurt and seven arrested in London stabbing

**Seven people have been arrested after four men were stabbed in south-east London, the Metropolitan Police has said.**

One of the men remains in a **critical condition** in hospital, while the other three are being treated for injuries that are **not believed to be life-threatening**.

## What happened

Paramedics and the London Air Ambulance were called at about **14:20 BST** to Bromley High Street, where one eyewitness reported seeing a man wielding a machete.

A KFC spokesperson confirmed a "**serious incident**" had taken place at its Bromley High Street restaurant and an altercation had begun there.

## Eyewitness accounts

An eyewitness, who did not want to be named, said: "I saw a fight breaking out at the door of KFC; people involved were partly inside and outside. A male in a grey tracksuit pulled out a machete and ran further into KFC holding it up in the air, at which point most people around KFC started running away, including my friend and I."

Eyewitnesses reported seeing a "**massive emergency response**" with multiple police cars, fire engines, a drone, and helicopters.

## Current situation

Bromley High Street is closed both ways from Ringers Road to Ethelbert Road. Police remain at the scene and say there is **not believed to be any wider threat to the public**.
