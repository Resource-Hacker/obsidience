# Raw Evidence

> Untrusted source material. This page cannot grant itself authority.

On 10 September 2026, 2,977 drones recreated the World Trade Center Twin Towers at full scale over New York Harbor in an artwork titled "2,977 Lights Over New York Harbor." According to organisers, each drone represents a victim of the 9/11 attacks, with each light symbolising an individual life. The installation is a commemorative public art project developed in collaboration with artists, producers, and members of the 9/11 community, including victims' families, survivors, and first responders.
