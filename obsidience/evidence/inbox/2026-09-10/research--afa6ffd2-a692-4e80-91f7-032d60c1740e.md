**Ex-Tory campaign chief pleads guilty over election bet**

*BBC News — 10 September 2026, 11:03 GMT*

Anthony Lee passed confidential information to his wife Laura Lee about when the 2024 election would be held.

Lee, a former top adviser to Prime Minister David Cameron, pleaded guilty at Southwark Crown Court to passing on a report that the 2024 UK general election would take place on 4 July 2024.

The information came from the Conservative Party's central polling team and was shared on 12 May 2024, two days before the election was announced.

Lee was sentenced to two years in prison and ordered to pay £600,000 in fines.

The court heard Lee admitted receiving the confidential polling report from campaign boss Kemi Badenoch on 12 May 2024, then passing it to his wife Laura Lee on a Friday evening.

Laura Lee later sent the same information to her husband, also a former political adviser, on a Tuesday night. Both received the same polling report on 16 May 2024.

Lee's defence counsel said the information had already been leaked to the press by the time it reached them.

The prosecution argued the offence lay in the passing of information *before* it was made public, regardless of whether it would ultimately be released.

A second defendant, Michael Lewis, the former head of the Conservative Party's central polling team, is due to appear at the same court next month on similar charges.
