**Four takeaways from Trump's speech at the first Republican midterms convention** | BBC News | 10 Sep 2026 | source://693de623-4e35-4cf5-bb22-ded7115d7b44

### 1. Preaching to the MAGA choir
The venue in Dallas was filled with diehard Trump loyalists in red MAGA gear. The old-style Republican establishment was notably absent — Senator Susan Collins and other moderate Republicans in competitive races stayed away. This raised questions about the effectiveness of an unprecedented midterm convention for reaching swing voters. [1](source://693de623-4e35-4cf5-bb22-ded7115d7b44)

### 2. It's still Trump's party
Trump acknowledged he remains the party's best turnout driver. Telling the crowd: "I'm asking you to pretend that I'm on the ballot." He did not appear in a hurry to yield the spotlight, though Vice-President JD Vance and Secretary of State Marco Rubio were frequently mentioned as potential 2028 successors. [2](source://45bd38fe-8c73-4360-bb95-2f68f92c57d2)

### 3. A gamble on affordability
Trump acknowledged Americans might wait longer for relief from high fuel prices, arguing short-term pain is justified to prevent Tehran from developing nuclear weapons. Polls show cost of living is a top issue, and the war's drag on his approval rating has hit a low of 33% among registered voters per a Financial Times / Focaldata poll. [2](source://45bd38fe-8c73-4360-bb95-2f68f92c57d2)

### 4. Trump makes $5,000 payout
Trump offered to pay every US adult $5,000 if Republicans win both chambers of Congress — with the caveat the money must be spent in the US. The plan drew raucous applause despite Democratic calls it an "empty promise" and legal questions, as the payout would cost roughly $1.3 trillion. [2](source://45bd38fe-8c73-4360-bb95-2f68f92c57d2)
