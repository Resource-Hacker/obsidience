# Five dead and 86 missing as 'heartbreaking' fire breaks out on Philippines ferry

Rescuers are searching for survivors after a vessel travelling from the mainland to a popular tourist destination caught fire, killing five people and leaving 86 missing.

The incident occurred on a Philippines ferry carrying passengers from the mainland to a tourist destination. The vessel caught fire, prompting a search operation for survivors.

The BBC reports the situation as "heartbreaking" with ongoing rescue efforts to locate those still missing.
