# Esther Ranzen says she is too fragile to travel to Switzerland to end life

**Source:** BBC News
**Published:** 10 September 2026, 16:37:18 UTC
**Reporting URL:** https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/clyk61jpgjjo
**Source citation:** source://f27fdd56-8c27-49e6-81e8-ed1e7fcab4b4

> The broadcaster and campaigner says she missed her chance to go before living with cancer became "unbearable."

Esther Ranzen has said she is too physically fragile to travel to Switzerland to access assisted dying, despite her wish to end her life. Ranzen has been living with cancer and describes the condition as having become "unbearable". She reports that she missed an opportunity to travel for assisted dying prior to the point when her cancer made the journey impossible. The material is presented as a report of Ranzen's personal account; it does not provide independent confirmation of any medical or legal assessment.
