# Raw Evidence

A federal judge in Miami has denied bail to dual US-UK citizens Andrew and Tristan Tate, ruling their wealth and frequent international travel "constitute quintessential flight risks."  Custody began in July 2025 following new UK charges. They face 59 charges in the UK — including rape, sex trafficking, and indecent images of a child, spanning 2010 to 2017 — and the UK must submit a full extradition request by 16 September 2026.

US Magistrate Judge Lauren Louis ruled that despite prior bail compliance in UK and Romania proceedings, the Tates possess an "exceptional capacity to flee the jurisdiction."  The UK Embassy argued custody is needed to prevent witness interference.  The brothers, who deny all charges, described jail conditions as a small, hot cell with limited sunlight and exercise; Tristan Tate reportedly lost 14 pounds (6.4kg) while detained.
