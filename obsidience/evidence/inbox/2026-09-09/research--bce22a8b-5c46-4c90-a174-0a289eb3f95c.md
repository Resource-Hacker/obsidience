# Miliband rejects chief rabbi's claim British Jews in greater danger after sanctions move

**Date:** Wed, 09 Sep 2026 13:28:58 GMT

**BBC News** reports that the UK Foreign Secretary rejects the Chief Rabbi's claim that sanctions on Israeli settlements could put British Jews in greater danger.

The Chief Rabbi had raised concerns about the potential impact of sanctions measures on Israeli settlements on the safety of British Jews. Foreign Secretary Miliband disagreed with this assessment.
