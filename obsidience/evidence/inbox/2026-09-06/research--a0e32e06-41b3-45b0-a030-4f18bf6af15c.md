## 7. UN adopts new world map reflecting Africa's size
The UN General Assembly voted to replace the standard world map with one that better reflects Africa's size. The Togo-sponsored "Correct the Map" resolution passed with 164 nations, including France and the UK; the US was the only vote against, six abstaining. The non-binding decision challenges the Mercator projection, which critics say minimises equatorial countries, after the African Union adopted the Equal Earth alternative.
