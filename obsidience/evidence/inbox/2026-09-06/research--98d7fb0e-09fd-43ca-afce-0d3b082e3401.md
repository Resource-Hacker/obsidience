## 10. UN votes to adopt new world map reflecting Africa's size
The UN General Assembly voted to replace the traditional Mercator world map with one that more accurately reflects Africa's size. The Togo-sponsored "Correct the Map" resolution passed with 164 votes, with the US the only nation against and six abstentions. The vote is non-binding, but the AU adopted the Equal Earth projection earlier this year, and France said it would stop using the Mercator map. Reported 4-5 Sept 2026.
