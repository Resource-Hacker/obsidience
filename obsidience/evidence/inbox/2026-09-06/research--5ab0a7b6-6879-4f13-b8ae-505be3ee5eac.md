## 8. UN votes to adopt new world map showing Africa's true size
Report date: 4 Sep 2026. https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ce30vp55dnlo — source://e90120e0-ce2c-4928-9da7-0e24654fe9a7
The UN General Assembly voted to replace the traditional Mercator world map with one that more accurately reflects Africa's size. The Togo-sponsored "Correct the Map" resolution passed with the support of 164 nations, including France and the UK; the US was the only country to vote against, with six abstentions. The vote is non-binding but is expected to change the maps used by institutions worldwide, moving away from a 16th-century projection that shrinks equatorial regions.
