## 9. UN votes to adopt new world map for Africa

The UN General Assembly voted on 4 September to adopt a new world map that more accurately reflects Africa's size, with the Togo-sponsored "Correct the Map" resolution backed by 164 nations and only the US voting against. The non-binding vote promotes the Equal Earth projection, adopted by the African Union in February, over the distorted Mercator map. Reported 4-5 September 2026.
