This collection holds durable research requested by the owner, with direct
Source citations, reporting dates, attribution, uncertainty and useful links.
[Darwin](/Agents/Darwin/Darwin.md) can answer Question or Learn requests;
[Alexandria](/Agents/Alexandria/Alexandria.md) reconciles cited findings with accepted
Knowledge through [Ingest](/Tasks/ingest.md) and the normal Review owner.

Publication, event and capture time remain distinct. One publisher's account
does not establish independent confirmation. Capture or handoff alone does not
establish wiki publication.
